Questions

Good to know

Are these drawing sheets free?

Yes. Print as many as you like for your family or your class.

What size are they?

US Letter, with a margin all round, so they print on A4 too. Choose “Fit to page” if your printer asks.

How do I use a sheet?

Put it beside your paper and draw one numbered step at a time: the dark lines are the new ones, the faded ones are done. The last picture shows the drawing finished.

Who are they for?

Kids from about six, on their own or with a grown-up beside them, and anyone starting to draw. Each path starts easy and gets harder.

See every line drawn first

In Paper Coach, Lina draws each line of a drawing on the screen, then waits while you draw it on paper. The first lessons of every path are free.
