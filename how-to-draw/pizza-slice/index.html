How to Draw a Pizza Slice

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 10 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 10steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a pizza slice in 10 steps: draw the top of the crust, draw the bottom of the crust, draw the left side, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 10
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

A pizza slice is really a triangle with a thick, rounded crust across the top. You build the crust first, hang the cheese below it in a big V, and set five pepperoni in rows that shrink toward the tip. Three colors finish it, and the whole thing comes together in a few minutes.

Tips

Draw a pizza slice well

Tips for drawing a pizza slice

  • Keep the gap between the two crust arches small and even, so the crust looks like a thick band instead of a thin line.
  • Round both crust ends like the ends of a sausage. That gives each side line a clear place to start.
  • Before drawing the sides, pick a tip point straight below the middle of the crust and aim both lines at it.
  • Let the pepperoni follow the shape: two wide apart near the crust, two closer together, then one near the tip.
  • Color the yellow cheese around the circles first, then fill the pepperoni red, so each color stays inside its own lines.

Common mistakes

  • The tip lands off to one side, so the slice leans. Find the middle of the crust and mark the tip directly below it before you draw either side.
  • Pepperoni bump into the crust or the side lines. Leave a thin strip of cheese around every circle, and set the last one in the middle, where the slice is narrow.
  • The crust is a single arch with no thickness. Draw a second arch underneath the first and join them with round ends, like a curved sausage.

Make it your own

  • Move the tip lower for a long, skinny slice, or higher for a short, wide one.
  • Draw two or three slices side by side, each with its tip pointing down, like slices lifted from the same pie.
  • Change the count: try three big pepperoni or seven small ones, still with fewer toward the tip.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the cheese look like it hangs from the crust?

Start both side lines right at the crust's round ends and slant them inward to one point. The wide top and narrow tip make the cheese look like it droops down from the crust.

Why does the crust need two arches?

One arch is only an outline. The second arch underneath gives the crust thickness, so it reads as the puffy edge of the slice and gets its own orange color.

How big should the pepperoni be?

Small enough that two fit side by side under the crust, with cheese between them and at both sides. Every circle needs a little yellow showing all around it.

How do I fit five pepperoni without crowding the tip?

Put them in three rows: two near the crust, two a bit closer together below, and one alone near the tip. Each row matches how narrow the slice is there.

How long does it take to draw a pizza slice?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
