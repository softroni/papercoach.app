How to Draw a Cauldron

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a cauldron in 11 steps: draw the ladle, draw the rim, draw the brew, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Fantasy Objects path in Paper Coach

This cauldron is built from two ovals, a round belly and three short legs, with a ladle leaning out and a fire burning underneath. Most of it is simple curves, so it is a calm drawing to share with a child. Bubbles and color turn it into a pot of bright green brew.

Tips

Draw a cauldron well

Tips for drawing a cauldron

  • Start the ladle handle near the top of your page. The rim oval wraps around it, so leaving room above helps.
  • Make the rim oval about twice as wide as it is tall. A flatter oval makes the pot look like you are viewing it from the side.
  • Keep the brew oval a little inside the rim on every side, so a thin band of rim shows all the way around.
  • Let the belly swell out past both ends of the rim, then curve back in near the bottom where the legs go.
  • Draw the flames in the gaps between the legs and keep them below the belly, so the fire sits under the pot.

Common mistakes

  • The belly comes out the same width as the rim, so the cauldron looks like a bucket. Push each side of the belly outward past the rim before rounding it toward the bottom.
  • The legs are long and thin, and the pot looks like it is on stilts. Keep each leg short, about as tall as the flames, and a little wider at the bottom.
  • All five bubbles end up on the brew and the scene looks crowded. Put three bubbles on the brew and float the other two above the rim, the higher one smallest.

Make it your own

  • Color the brew blue or orange instead of green, with pale bubbles to match.
  • Add more floating bubbles rising above the rim, each one a little smaller than the last.
  • Make the flames taller, with four tips each, for a cauldron that is boiling hard.
  • Lean the ladle to the left instead and let the rim wrap around it from the other side.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the cauldron look round and not flat?

Use an oval for the rim instead of a straight line, and make the belly bulge wider than that oval. The curve on both shows the pot is round.

Why does the ladle come first?

The rim and brew both curve around the handle. Drawing the handle first tells you where those ovals start and keeps the ladle looking like it sits inside the pot.

How do I draw the flames so they look like fire?

Give each flame three pointed tips and a wider bottom. Then draw a smaller flame inside it, and color the outside red and the inside yellow.

Where should the middle leg go?

Put it under the center of the belly, between the left and right legs. It can be a little shorter, since it stands toward the back of the pot.

How long does it take to draw a cauldron?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
