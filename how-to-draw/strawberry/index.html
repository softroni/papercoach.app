How to Draw a Strawberry

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 9 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 9steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a strawberry in 9 steps: draw the left leaf, draw the right leaf, draw the stem, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 9
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Fruits path in Paper Coach

This strawberry starts at the top. You draw the leafy hat first, then hang a heart-shaped berry under it. Building the leaves first gives the berry a clear place to start and stop, so the whole fruit stays in balance.

Tips

Draw a strawberry well

Tips for drawing a strawberry

  • Make the left and right leaves mirror each other. If one curls higher, the hat will look tilted.
  • Keep the stem short, about as tall as the middle leaf is wide, so it doesn't steal attention from the berry.
  • Start the berry lines where the leaf edges end at the tips. That way the hat sits neatly on the berry.
  • Make the berry about three times as tall as the leafy hat. That keeps the fruit looking plump.
  • Give the bottom a soft, rounded point, not a sharp one. A gentle tip reads as fruit, not as a heart card.

Common mistakes

  • The berry starts too far inside the leaves, so the hat hangs off the sides like a wide brim. Begin each side of the berry right at a leaf tip, then curve outward before heading down.
  • The middle leaf is drawn wide and round, so it looks like a blob instead of a leaf. Keep it a narrow V, with both lines meeting in a point just below the other leaves.
  • Red is colored over the seeds, so they disappear. Color around the six seeds with red, then fill them in black last.

Make it your own

  • Draw two strawberries side by side, one a little smaller and set slightly lower, so they look like a pair.
  • Make the berry taller and narrower for a long, slim strawberry, keeping the same leafy hat on top.
  • Tilt the whole strawberry to one side, with the stem leaning the same way, so it looks like it is falling.
  • Draw a small cluster of three strawberries touching, as practice for the overlapping fruit bowl at the end of the path.

Questions

Good to know

Why does this strawberry start with the leaves instead of the berry?

The leaves set the width and position of the top. Once the hat is in place, the berry's two curves have clear start points at the leaf tips, which makes the heart shape easier to get even.

How do I make the berry look like a heart without it looking like a valentine?

Skip the dip at the top, since the leaves cover that part. Round out the sides fully and soften the bottom point.

How many seeds should a strawberry drawing have?

This one uses six, which is enough to say strawberry without crowding the berry. Spread them out so no two sit right next to each other.

How can I make my strawberry look even on both sides?

Draw the left and right leaves at the same angle, then check that the berry's lowest point lines up under the stem.

How long does it take to draw a strawberry?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
