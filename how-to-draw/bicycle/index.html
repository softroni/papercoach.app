How to Draw a Bicycle

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a bicycle in 11 steps: draw the back wheel, draw the front wheel, draw the hubs, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Wheels path in Paper Coach

A bicycle looks tricky, but it is mostly two circles joined by straight lines. You start with the wheels and their center dots, then build the frame as two triangles between them. A fork, flat handlebars and a small wedge seat finish it, so you can see how every bike is put together.

Tips

Draw a bicycle well

Tips for drawing a bicycle

  • Keep both wheels the same size and sitting on the same level, with a gap between them a little narrower than one wheel.
  • Put each hub dot right in the middle of its wheel. The frame lines and the fork all aim at those dots.
  • The bottom of the seat tube, where the pedals go, sits between the wheels, level with the hubs.
  • Keep the top bar level. The seat tube, the bar up from the pedals and the fork all slant.
  • Draw the frame with light lines first. Once both triangles close neatly, go over them darker.

Common mistakes

  • The second wheel comes out bigger or smaller than the first. Hold your pencil against the back wheel to measure its width, then mark that width before drawing the front wheel.
  • The fork stops at the top of the front wheel, so the wheel looks loose. Carry the fork line all the way down to the front hub dot, so the wheel hangs from it.
  • The back frame lines float near the hub and leave gaps. Start both lines exactly on the back hub dot and end each one touching the seat tube.

Make it your own

  • Add a few straight spokes running from each hub out to the rim.
  • Draw a ground line under both wheels so the bicycle stands on a road.
  • Make a small kid's bike by drawing smaller wheels closer together and a shorter seat tube.
  • Trace the frame in your favorite color so it stands out from the wheels.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make a bicycle frame look right?

Think of it as two triangles. The back one joins the back hub to the top and bottom of the seat tube. The front one is the seat tube, the level top bar and the slanted bar from the pedals.

Why draw the wheels before the frame?

The wheels set the size and spacing of the whole bike. Their hub dots give you fixed points to connect the frame and fork to, so the lines land in the right places.

How do I draw round wheels freehand?

Draw one half of the circle, then the other, with light strokes. Turn your paper if that helps your hand curve. Fix any flat spots before you go over it darker.

Where do the handlebars go on a bicycle?

They sit on top of the fork, just above the front corner of the frame. Draw them as one flat line across, like the top of a T.

How long does it take to draw a bicycle?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
