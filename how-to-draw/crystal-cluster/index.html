How to Draw a Crystal Cluster

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a crystal cluster in 7 steps: draw the rock, draw the middle crystal, draw the next two, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Fantasy Objects path in Paper Coach

This crystal cluster is five tall, pointed crystals growing out of one low rock. The trick that makes them look solid is simple: one line down the middle of each crystal, then a light color on the left half and a dark color on the right. Once you see how that works, you can use it on gems, crowns and anything else with sharp sides.

Tips

Draw a crystal cluster well

Tips for drawing a crystal cluster

  • Keep the rock wide and low, about twice as wide as it is tall, so the crystals have room to fan out across its top.
  • Make the middle crystal the tallest. Each pair after it gets shorter, so the outer two are only about half its height.
  • Start each middle line at the crystal's tip and run it straight down to where the crystal meets the rock.
  • Tilt the crystals out from the center like a hand of cards. The middle one stands straight, the next pair leans a little, the outer pair leans more.
  • Color every left side blue before any purple goes on. Doing all of one side at once keeps the light coming from the same place.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing all five crystals the same height, so the cluster looks like a fence. Step the heights down from the center out: tallest in the middle, medium next to it, shortest on the outside.
  • Putting the blue on the left of some crystals and the right of others. Light comes from one side only. Every crystal gets blue on its left half and purple on its right half.
  • Letting the crystals float above the rock with a gap underneath. Bring the bottom of each crystal down until it touches or tucks into the rock's curved top.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: try light green with dark green, or pink with deep red, keeping the lighter one on the left.
  • Draw seven crystals instead of five, adding a tiny pair at the very ends that leans out almost sideways.
  • Move the light to the right side instead, so the purple goes on the left halves and the blue on the right.
  • Draw two or three small clusters on separate rocks side by side to make a crystal cave floor.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make a crystal look 3D?

Split it with one line from the tip straight down the middle. Color one half light and the other half dark. The two tones make it read as a solid shape with two flat faces.

Why does each crystal have a line down the middle?

That line is the edge where two faces of the crystal meet. Without it, the crystal looks flat like a paper cutout. With it, you have two sides to light and shade.

What shape is a crystal when you draw it?

Think of a fat pencil: a long, straight-sided shape that ends in a point at the top and sits flat into the rock at the bottom.

How do I keep the crystals from looking messy?

Build from the center out in pairs, matching the lean and height of each pair on both sides. The balance makes the cluster look neat and planned.

How long does it take to draw a crystal cluster?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
