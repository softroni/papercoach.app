How to Draw a UFO

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 8 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 8steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a UFO in 8 steps: draw the top curve, draw the underside, draw the dome, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 8
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Space path in Paper Coach

This flying saucer starts as a lens shape: two curves that meet at sharp points on each side. A half-circle dome sits on top, three lights run along the side, and a beam drops to the ground. It is a quick space drawing, and the beam makes it look like it is landing.

Tips

Draw a UFO well

Tips for drawing a UFO

  • Mark the left and right points first with two small dots at the same height. Then both curves have a clear place to start and stop.
  • Make the underside a little shallower than the top curve. That small difference keeps the saucer from looking like a plain oval.
  • Make the dome a little under half as wide as the saucer, centered between the two points.
  • Space the three lights by placing the middle one first, under the dome, then one on each side at equal distances.
  • Let the beam get wider as it goes down, until its flat bottom is a little over half as wide as the saucer.

Common mistakes

  • The ends of the saucer come out round, so it looks like an egg lying on its side. Bring the top and bottom curves together at a sharp point on the left and on the right, like a lens.
  • The dome is drawn off to one side or floats above the saucer. Start and end the half circle right on the top curve, with equal space on its left and right.
  • The beam leans to one side because it starts away from the middle. Begin both slanted sides just below the center light and slant them out by the same amount.

Make it your own

  • Stretch the saucer wider and keep the dome small for a sleek, fast-looking ship.
  • Draw five small lights instead of three, still in an even row along the side.
  • Leave out the beam to show the UFO flying across the page instead of landing.
  • Draw two or three saucers in different sizes, smaller ones higher up, so they look farther away.

Questions

Good to know

How do I draw a UFO shape that looks right?

Think of a lens: two curves joined at sharp points on the left and right. Make the bottom curve a little shallower than the top, then set a half-circle dome in the middle on top.

How big should the dome be on a flying saucer?

A little under half of the saucer's width, as in this lesson. Much bigger makes it look top heavy, and much smaller gets lost on the saucer.

How do I draw the light beam under a UFO?

Start just under the middle of the saucer. Draw two lines slanting outward as they go down, then join them with a flat line at the bottom, like a lampshade.

How can I make a UFO drawing look like it is glowing?

Use bright colors against the gray saucer: orange for the round lights and yellow for the beam. The contrast makes those parts look lit up.

How long does it take to draw a UFO?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
