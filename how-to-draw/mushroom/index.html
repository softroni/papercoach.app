How to Draw a Mushroom

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a mushroom in 11 steps: draw the left side, draw the right side, draw the rim, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Plants path in Paper Coach

This mushroom starts as a wide dome made of two curves that meet at the top. Three spots sit on the cap, a stem drops from the middle of the rim, and short gill lines fan out underneath. It is a quick drawing, and the gills are what make it look real.

Tips

Draw a mushroom well

Tips for drawing a mushroom

  • Make the cap about twice as wide as it is tall, so it reads as a dome and not a ball.
  • Put the top of both cap curves at the same point, so the two halves meet in one smooth peak.
  • Keep the stem narrower than the cap at the rim, about a third of its width, then let it widen toward the foot.
  • Start each gill line near the stem and pull it out toward the rim, so all six seem to come from one center.
  • Leave a little red showing between each spot and the rim so the spots look like they sit on the cap.

Common mistakes

  • The rim is drawn as a flat, straight line, so the cap looks like a half circle cut with scissors. Let the rim lift a little in the middle, like a gentle smile turned over, so the cap curves around the stem.
  • The three spots are all the same size and lined up in a row. Make the top spot the biggest and place the two smaller ones lower, one on each side, like a loose triangle.
  • The gill lines run straight down, parallel, like a fence. Tilt them so they spread apart as they get closer to the rim, the left ones leaning left and the right ones leaning right.

Make it your own

  • Draw a second, smaller mushroom beside the first, with a shorter stem and a cap half the width.
  • Try more spots, five or six small ovals of different sizes scattered over the cap.
  • Color the cap brown or orange instead of red and keep the spots cream for a softer look.
  • Make the stem taller and thinner for a mushroom that stands up high on its round foot.

Questions

Good to know

How do I get the mushroom cap round and even?

Draw it in two halves from the same top point. Make the left curve first, then mirror it on the right, checking that both ends land at the same height before you join them with the rim.

What are the lines under a mushroom cap?

Those are gills, thin folds under the cap. In a drawing, a few short lines fanning out from the stem toward the rim are enough to show them.

Where should the stem attach to the cap?

Right at the middle of the rim. If it sits off to one side, the mushroom looks like it is tipping over.

How can a young child draw this mushroom?

Call the cap a rainbow and the spots eggs. A grown-up can draw the dome first while the child adds the spots, stem and gill lines.

How long does it take to draw a mushroom?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
