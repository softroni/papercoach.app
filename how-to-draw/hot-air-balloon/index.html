How to Draw a Hot Air Balloon

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 9 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 9steps

Quick answer

Draw a hot air balloon in 9 steps: draw the left side, draw the right side, close the bottom, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 9
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the In the Air path in Paper Coach

This hot air balloon starts as one big teardrop with a flat line across the bottom. Two long curves split the bag into three stripes, and two short lines lead down to a little tub-shaped basket. The shapes are simple and bold, so it's a good first drawing of things that fly.

Tips

Draw a hot air balloon well

Tips for drawing a hot air balloon

  • Start both side curves from the same point at the top. That way the balloon comes to a soft point instead of a gap or a bump.
  • Let the sides bulge out wide near the top, then pull in toward the bottom. The widest part should sit about a third of the way down.
  • Keep the bottom line short, about a quarter as wide as the widest part of the balloon. A small mouth makes it look like a teardrop.
  • Draw each stripe curve from the top point down to the mouth. Bend each one the same way as the side next to it, so the stripes look round.
  • Make the basket about as wide as the mouth, or a little wider. Keep it small next to the balloon so the bag still looks big and full of air.

Common mistakes

  • The two sides don't match, so the balloon leans or one side looks flat. Draw the right side as a mirror of the left. Glance across at the left curve while you draw so both bulge out the same amount.
  • The stripe lines are drawn straight, so the balloon looks flat like a paper cutout. Bow each stripe line outward, following the side closest to it. The middle stripe should be widest in the middle and narrow at both ends.
  • The skirt lines lean outward or never reach the basket, so the basket seems to float. Start each skirt line at an end of the mouth and slant it inward. Stop it right where the top of the basket begins.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: try a yellow outer pair with a red middle stripe, or pick any two colors you like.
  • Draw two or three balloons at different sizes. Make the faraway ones smaller and higher on the page.
  • Add more stripe curves to split the bag into five thinner stripes, and switch between two colors as you go.
  • Make the balloon rounder and fatter, or tall and thin, to give each one its own shape.

Questions

Good to know

How do I draw a hot air balloon shape that isn't lopsided?

Mark a small dot for the top and a short line for the mouth first, lined up under it. Then draw each side curve between them, bulging out the same width on both sides.

Why does my balloon look like a circle instead of a teardrop?

The bottom is probably too wide or too round. Bring the sides in closer toward the bottom and close it with a short, straight line.

How do I make the stripes look curved around the balloon?

Draw them from the top point to the mouth, bending outward like the sides. Curved lines that follow the edge make the bag look round, not flat.

How big should the basket be compared to the balloon?

Keep it small. About as wide as the mouth and roughly a fifth as tall as the balloon looks right and shows how big the bag is.

How long does it take to draw a hot air balloon?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
