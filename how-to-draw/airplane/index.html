How to Draw an Airplane

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 9 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 9steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw an airplane in 9 steps: draw the top line, draw the belly, draw the near wing, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 9
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the In the Air path in Paper Coach

This airplane starts as one long tube with a rounded nose. You add a wing below the body, a second wing peeking above it, a tail fin at the back and a neat row of four windows. Because both wings point back the same way, the plane looks like it is flying forward.

Tips

Draw an airplane well

Tips for drawing an airplane

  • Keep the top line flat and long, about four times longer than the body is tall. A long tube reads as an airplane right away.
  • Make the nose a smooth, round curve, and let the back end narrow a little toward the tail.
  • Start the near wing under the middle of the body, not near the nose. That keeps the plane balanced.
  • Draw the far wing smaller than the near wing. Only its tip needs to show above the top line.
  • Space the four windows evenly, like beads on a string, and keep them all the same size.

Common mistakes

  • The two wings point in different directions, so the plane looks like it is turning or broken. Sweep both wings back toward the tail at the same slant, as if they were parallel lines.
  • The tail fin sits in the middle of the body or leans toward the nose. Put the fin at the very back end and tilt its top edge back, toward the tail.
  • The windows drift up and down or run onto the wing. Line them up in one straight row along the side, above the near wing, before you color.

Make it your own

  • Draw a few puffy cloud shapes around the plane so it looks high up in the sky.
  • Make the body longer and add more round windows to turn it into a big passenger jet.
  • Swap the colors: try a blue body with yellow wings and tail fin.
  • Draw a second, much smaller airplane far away in the corner to show distance.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the airplane body look like a tube and not a flat rectangle?

Keep the top line straight, then round the nose with one smooth curve and run the belly back to the tail. That rounded front end is what makes the body feel like a tube.

Why does one wing go below the body and the other above it?

The near wing hangs below the body because it is closer to you. The far wing sits on the other side, so you only see its tip poking up above the top line.

How big should the windows be?

Each window should be a small circle, much shorter than the body is tall, so there is space above and below it. Four in a row should fit along the front half of the body.

How can a young child draw this airplane?

Let them draw a long hot dog shape for the body, then add two triangles for wings and one for the tail. Four dots for windows finish it nicely.

How long does it take to draw an airplane?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
