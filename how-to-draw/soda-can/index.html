How to Draw a Soda Can

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a soda can in 11 steps: draw the back of the rim, close the rim, draw the ring, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

A soda can is a cylinder with a few extras on top. You start with a flat oval rim, tuck a thin ring and a small tab inside it, then drop the sides and wrap a label band around the middle. If your curves match, the can looks round and solid.

Tips

Draw a soda can well

Tips for drawing a soda can

  • Keep the rim oval wide and flat, about three and a half times wider than it is tall. A flatter oval makes the can look like you are seeing it from the side.
  • Draw the inner oval close to the outer one, so the ring stays thin and even all the way around.
  • Keep the tab small, about a quarter of the lid's width, and set it inside the inner oval so it lies flat on the lid.
  • Make the two sides the same length and keep them parallel once they turn straight down. That keeps the can standing upright.
  • Bend the bottom curve and both band curves the same way as the front of the rim: the band about as deep as the rim, the bottom a little deeper. Matching curves make the can look round.

Common mistakes

  • The bottom of the can is drawn as a straight line, so the can looks flat like a box. Let the bottom sag downward in the middle, the same way the front of the rim curves.
  • The band curves bend upward or bend more than the bottom, so the label looks twisted. Copy the shape of the bottom curve for both band lines, and keep the two lines evenly spaced across the middle.
  • The sides go straight down right from the rim, so the top looks pinched. Start each side with a short slope outward from the rim's ends, then turn straight down.

Make it your own

  • Change the colors to make your own flavor: try green or orange for the can body and a different color for the band.
  • Move the band higher or lower, or make it wider, to change the look of the label.
  • Draw two or three cans side by side, with their bottoms sitting on the same line, like a row on a shelf.
  • Draw a taller, thinner can by stretching the sides longer while keeping the same rim.

Questions

Good to know

Why does my soda can look flat instead of round?

Usually the bottom or the band is drawn straight. Give every line that goes across the can a gentle downward curve, matching the front of the rim.

How do I draw the ring on top of a soda can?

Draw a second, slightly smaller oval just inside the rim. Keep the gap between the two ovals narrow and even, so it reads as a thin ring.

Where does the pull tab go on a soda can?

Place it on the lid, inside the inner oval. Draw it as a small rounded shape lying flat, not standing up.

How is drawing a soda can like drawing a cylinder?

It is a cylinder with an oval top, straight sides and a curved bottom. The ring, tab and label band are details added on that simple shape.

How long does it take to draw a soda can?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
