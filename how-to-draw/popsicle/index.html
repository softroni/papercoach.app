How to Draw a Popsicle

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a popsicle in 7 steps: draw the left side, draw the bottom and right, draw the bite, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

This popsicle is a tall block with a rounded top, a stick underneath and a bite missing from one corner. The bite is just two little scoops, so it is easy to get right. It is a good second treat to draw, and a fun one to share with a child who loves summer snacks.

Tips

Draw a popsicle well

Tips for drawing a popsicle

  • Make the block about twice as tall as it is wide. A taller shape reads as a popsicle, while a squat one looks more like a bar of soap.
  • Keep the two side lines straight and parallel. Only the top corners should curve.
  • Stop the right side a little below the top. That gap is where the bite goes, so leave room for two scoops.
  • Draw the stick about as wide as a third of the popsicle, and shorter than the block, so the treat stays the star.
  • Place the two grooves in the lower half, close together and the same length, with space all around them.

Common mistakes

  • Closing the outline all the way around before drawing the bite. Leave the top right open after the right side. Then the scoops fill that gap and connect back to the start.
  • Letting the grooves touch the edges of the popsicle. Keep each groove short and floating in the middle, with a clear gap to the sides and bottom.
  • Drawing the stick as a closed box with a flat bottom. Start at the bottom edge, go down, curve around a round end and come back up to the popsicle.

Make it your own

  • Color the popsicle orange, green or purple to make a different flavor, and keep the stick cream.
  • Move the bite to the top left corner by stopping the left side early instead.
  • Draw two popsicles side by side, one with a bite and one without, in different colors.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the bite look like a bite?

Use two small, round scoops next to each other, curving inward into the popsicle. Rounded dips look like teeth marks, while a straight cut just looks like a missing corner.

What are the two lines on the popsicle for?

They are grooves, like the ridges pressed into a real frozen pop. They give the flat shape a little texture and make it look less like a plain rectangle.

Why does my popsicle look lopsided?

Usually one side is longer or leans. Check that both sides go straight down and end at the same height before you draw the bottom.

Can a young child draw this popsicle?

Yes. It is mostly straight lines and a few curves. A parent can draw the outline and let the child add the bite, the grooves and the color.

How long does it take to draw a popsicle?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
