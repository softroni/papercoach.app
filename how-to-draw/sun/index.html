How to Draw a Sun

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 6 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

Quick answer

Draw a sun in 6 steps: draw the left side, draw the right side, draw the top and bottom rays, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 6
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Sky & Weather path in Paper Coach

Step by step

Each picture shows the lines so far: the faded ones are done, the dark ones are this step’s.

  1. Step 1: Draw the left side

    The sun is a big circle. From the top, curve around the left side down to the bottom.

  2. Step 2: Draw the right side

    Now close the circle. From the top again, curve around the right side.

  3. Step 3: Draw the top and bottom rays

    Rays are short straight lines. Draw one above the sun and one below, not touching it.

    Next, one ray on the left and one on the right.

  5. Step 5: Draw the slanted rays

    Four more rays fit in the gaps, slanting out like an X.

  6. Step 6: Color the sun

    Color the sun yellow.

Questions

Is a sun easy to draw?

Yes. This guide splits it into 6 small steps, each a line or two, so you only ever draw one new thing at a time.

How long does it take to draw a sun?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
