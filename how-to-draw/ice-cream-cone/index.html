How to Draw an Ice Cream Cone

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw an ice cream cone in 11 steps: draw the cone, draw the right side, draw the skirt, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

This ice cream cone stacks two scoops on a long, narrow V. The main skill here is the waffle pattern, where three slanted lines cross three more to make rows of little diamonds. The wavy skirt under each scoop matters too, because it makes the top scoop look like it rests on the one below.

Tips

Draw an ice cream cone well

Tips for drawing an ice cream cone

  • Make the V about twice as tall as it is wide across the top. A long cone leaves plenty of room for the waffle lines.
  • Keep the three bumps in each skirt about the same size. Sketch them lightly first if you want to check the spacing.
  • Start each side of the first scoop at the outer end of its skirt. Lean both sides in so the scoop is narrower at the top.
  • Make the second skirt a little narrower than the first, so the top scoop fits on the bottom one.
  • Space the waffle lines evenly across the cone. Equal gaps give you diamonds that match in size.

Common mistakes

  • The waffle lines run past the edges of the cone. Start and stop every line right on the sides of the V. Slow down as you reach each edge.
  • The waffle lines form long slanted boxes instead of diamonds. Give the second set the same slant as the first, only leaning the opposite way.
  • The dome looks flat, so the top scoop seems squashed. Begin at one end of the top skirt, curve up high, and come down at the other end. Aim for a tall half circle.

Make it your own

  • Trade the scoop colors, with light green on the bottom and pink on top.
  • Draw four waffle lines each way instead of three for smaller diamonds.
  • Add a third scoop by repeating the skirt and sides before you draw the dome.
  • Make the V shorter and wider for a stubby cone.

Questions

Good to know

How do you draw a waffle pattern on an ice cream cone?

Draw three evenly spaced slanted lines from one side of the V to the other. Then add three more leaning the opposite way. The spaces where they cross become diamonds.

How do you make two scoops look stacked?

Give each scoop its own wavy skirt. The top skirt rests on the rounded top of the bottom scoop, so it looks like one scoop is sitting on the other.

Why does the bottom of each scoop have bumps?

The bumps look like soft ice cream spilling over the edge below it. A straight line there would make the scoop look like a stiff cap.

How big should the scoops be compared to the cone?

Make the first skirt as wide as the top of the cone, or a touch wider. Keep both scoops together a little shorter than the cone so it stays long.

How long does it take to draw an ice cream cone?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
