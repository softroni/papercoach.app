How to Draw a Mug

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 10 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 10steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a mug in 10 steps: draw the rim, close the rim, draw the inner oval, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 10
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

A mug is a cylinder you already know, plus one handle. You start at the top with two ovals, one inside the other, and the space between them shows how thick the mug is. Then two straight sides, a curved bottom and a C-shaped handle turn it into something you could drink from.

Tips

Draw a mug well

Tips for drawing a mug

  • Make the rim oval about three times wider than it is tall. A flat oval tells the eye you are looking down at the mug from a little above.
  • Keep the inner oval the same distance from the outer one all the way around, like a thin ring, so the edge looks even.
  • Let both sides drop straight down and stay parallel. Line them up with the far left and far right points of the rim oval.
  • Bend the bottom curve the same direction as the front of the rim. Matching curves make the mug look round instead of flat.
  • Start and end the handle on the mug's side, with the top end a little below the rim and the bottom end a little above the base.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the bottom as a straight line, so the mug looks like a flat box. Sag the bottom line downward in a gentle curve, a little deeper than the front curve of the rim.
  • Making the inner oval almost as big as the rim, so the edge disappears. Leave a clear gap between the two ovals, about the width of a pencil line or two.
  • Drawing the handle hole too small or crooked, so it looks like a solid lump. Follow the outside curve with a smaller C inside it, keeping a steady gap so the hole looks big enough for fingers.

Make it your own

  • Pick another mug color, like blue or yellow, and color the thick edge a lighter shade of the same color.
  • Fill the mug with something else instead of hot chocolate, such as pale tan coffee with milk or orange tea.
  • Make the sides taller and the rim oval narrower for a tall travel-style mug, keeping the handle the same shape.
  • Draw two mugs side by side, one a little smaller, for a pair of matching cups.

Questions

Good to know

Why does the mug start with an oval and not a circle?

A round rim seen from a little above looks squashed into an oval. A circle would mean looking straight down into the mug, and then you couldn't see its sides.

Why is the bottom curved if the mug sits flat?

The bottom is a circle too, seen at an angle. Its front half bends toward you, so the line curves down just like the front of the rim.

Which way should the handle face?

In this guide it is a backwards C on the right side. You can put it on the left as a normal C, as long as both ends touch the mug's side.

How do I show the drink inside the mug?

Color only the inner oval brown. The pink ring around it shows the mug's thick edge, so the drink looks like it sits inside.

How long does it take to draw a mug?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
