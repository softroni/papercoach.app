How to Draw a Train

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 16 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 16steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a train in 16 steps: draw the engine frame, draw the cab, draw the window, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 16
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Wheels path in Paper Coach

This train is made from simple shapes: two flat bars, a tall box for the cab, a long low boiler and a box for the freight car. Each bar has two arches with a wheel tucked under each one. Once the shapes are in place, you give every part its own bright color.

Tips

Draw a train well

Tips for drawing a train

  • Make the cab about twice as tall as the boiler. That height difference is what makes the engine look like a steam train.
  • Draw both bars at the same height on the page, so the engine and the freight car sit level with each other.
  • Leave a small gap between the engine bar and the car bar. The coupling dash goes right in that gap.
  • Make each arch a bit wider than the wheel you plan to draw, so the circle fits neatly inside it.
  • Start the smokestack narrow where it touches the boiler, then let the two sides lean outward as they go up.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing a fourth line along the bottom of the freight car box, so it looks like a second bar. Stop after up, across and down. The bar underneath already works as the bottom of the box.
  • Making the wheels so big that they bump into each other or poke far out of the arches. Keep each circle about as wide as its arch, with a clear space between the two wheels on each bar.
  • Placing the hubs off to one side, which makes the wheels look wobbly. Find the middle of each wheel first, then draw a small circle right on that spot.

Make it your own

  • Pick your own colors for the boiler, cab and freight car, but keep the frames dark so the bars stand apart from the boxes.
  • Add a second freight car behind the first, with its own bar, two arches, box, wheels and a coupling dash.
  • Stretch the boiler longer or make the cab taller to give your engine a different shape.
  • Give each car a different color so the whole train looks like a parade of boxes.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the engine look like a train and not a truck?

The long, low boiler with a round nose and the flowerpot smokestack on top do the work. Keep the cab tall and at the back, behind the boiler.

How do I draw the smokestack?

Draw two short lines that lean away from each other as they rise from the boiler, then close the top with a flat line. Narrow at the bottom, wide at the top.

How do I keep all four wheels the same size?

Draw all four arches the same width first. Then fit one circle inside each arch, so the arches guide the size of every wheel.

Where does the coupling go?

Draw one short dash between the front end of the car bar and the back end of the engine bar, at the same height as the bars.

How long does it take to draw a train?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
