How to Draw a Potion Bottle

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a potion bottle in 11 steps: draw the flask, finish the flask, draw the first ring, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Fantasy Objects path in Paper Coach

This potion bottle is a big round belly with a tall neck made of three gold rings stacked between short pieces of glass. A wavy line fills the flask with purple potion, and five pink bubbles float inside. If you can draw a circle with a flat bottom and a few rounded bands, you can draw this bottle.

Tips

Draw a potion bottle well

Tips for drawing a potion bottle

  • Make the belly much wider than the neck. If the neck is about one fifth as wide as the flask, the bottle looks round and heavy.
  • Draw both sides of the flask from the same spot under the neck, so the two curves match and the bottle doesn't lean.
  • Keep the three gold rings the same width and height. Matching rings make the neck look neat and stacked.
  • Put the wavy potion line a little above the middle of the flask. That leaves enough room under it for all five bubbles.
  • Let the cork stick out above the top ring, a bit narrower than the ring, so it looks pushed in.

Common mistakes

  • The flask comes out lopsided, with one side bulging more than the other. Before you draw the right side, look at the left curve and copy how far out it swings.
  • The neck pieces are so tall that the bottle looks like a vase. Keep each neck piece short, about the height of one ring. The flask should stay the biggest part.
  • All five bubbles are the same size and sit in a straight row. Make one bubble clearly bigger and low in the potion, then scatter the four small ones around it at different heights.

Make it your own

  • Fill the flask with a different potion color, like green or orange, and keep the blue glass above it.
  • Change how full the bottle is by moving the wavy line higher or lower in the flask.
  • Draw a row of three bottles with potions at different levels, like a shelf in a wizard's shop.
  • Try silver rings instead of gold, and a darker brown cork.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the flask look round and not like an oval?

Swing each side out wide, almost as wide as the flask is tall, then curve back in to a short flat bottom. The flat bottom helps it sit on the table.

Why does the potion line need to be wavy?

A wavy top makes the liquid look like it's moving inside the glass. A straight line can look like a stripe painted on the bottle instead.

How do I draw the rings on the neck?

Draw a flat band with rounded ends, a little wider than the neck. Then repeat it twice, each one the same size, with a short piece of neck between them.

How can a child draw this bottle more easily?

Start with the big round belly, then stack three rings like coins on top. Skip the small bubbles at first and add just the big one if five feel like too many.

How long does it take to draw a potion bottle?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
