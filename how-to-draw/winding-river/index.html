How to Draw a Winding River

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a winding river in 11 steps: draw the far line, draw the hills, draw the left bank, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Landscape path in Paper Coach

This river starts wide at the bottom of the page and shrinks to a single point on the far line. That narrowing is what makes the water look far away. You only need one straight line, a bumpy hill line, two curving banks and a circle, then four colors.

Tips

Draw a winding river well

Tips for drawing a winding river

  • Put the far line about a third of the way down the page. That leaves lots of room below for the river to curve and spread out.
  • Keep the hills low, no taller than a finger width above the far line, so they feel distant.
  • Start the two banks far apart at the bottom edge, about half the page width, so the river has room to shrink.
  • Make each bend smaller than the one below it. Big curves near you and small curves far away add depth.
  • Draw the sun off to one side, well above the hills, so it doesn't crowd the point where the banks meet.

Common mistakes

  • The banks stay the same distance apart all the way up, so the river looks like a flat ribbon. Let the gap between the banks get smaller after every bend until they touch on the far line.
  • The two banks meet below the far line or poke above it into the hills. Pick one spot on the far line first and aim both banks at that exact point.
  • The right bank curves the opposite way from the left, so the river pinches in the middle. Make the right bank bend the same direction as the left at each turn, like two lines following each other.

Make it your own

  • Flip the S so the river bends right first, then left, for a mirror view of the same valley.
  • Add a third bend by making smaller wiggles near the top, which makes the river feel even longer.
  • Move the sun low, just above the hills, and color it orange for an evening scene.
  • Color the far fields a lighter green than the near ones to push the distance back.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make a river look like it goes far away?

Draw it wide at the bottom and narrow at the top, ending in a point on the far line. Making the bends smaller as they go up helps too.

Why should the river be shaped like an S?

A curving river pulls your eye back and forth up the page, so it feels deeper than a straight one. The bends also hide part of the water, like a real valley.

Should I draw the left bank or the right bank first?

Start with the left bank all the way up. Then draw the right bank following its curves, so you can see how far apart they should be at each spot.

Is this river drawing easy enough for kids?

Yes. It uses only lines and one circle. A parent can draw the far line and a child can try the banks, aiming both at the same point.

How long does it take to draw a winding river?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
