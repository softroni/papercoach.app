How to Draw a Sunflower

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 12 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 12steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a sunflower in 12 steps: draw the middle, draw four petals, draw four more petals, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 12
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Plants path in Paper Coach

A sunflower is one big circle with petals all the way around it. You draw eight flame-shaped petals in front, then tuck eight small tent shapes behind them so the flower looks full. A tall stem and two wide leaves finish it, and four colors bring it to life.

Tips

Draw a sunflower well

Tips for drawing a sunflower

  • Make the middle circle large, about as wide as two petals are long. A big center is what makes it read as a sunflower.
  • Place the first four petals like the points of a compass: top, right, bottom, left. Then the gaps between them come out even.
  • Keep the back petals shorter than the front ones. Only their tips should show past the front petals.
  • Start each leaf stalk on the stem itself, so the leaves look attached and not floating beside it.
  • Put the right leaf lower than the left one. Uneven heights make the plant look natural.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the back petals as full flames, so they cover the front ones and the flower looks crowded. Draw only a small point peeking out of each gap, starting and ending where the front petals meet.
  • Starting the stem at the circle, so it cuts through the bottom petal. Begin the two stem lines at the tip of the bottom petal and run them straight down.
  • Making the eight front petals very different sizes, so the flower looks lopsided. Draw the first four the same length, then match the four in the gaps to them.

Make it your own

  • Draw a row of three sunflowers at different heights for a little garden.
  • Make the stem lean slightly to one side, as if the flower is turning toward the sun.
  • Dot the brown middle with small darker circles to show the seeds.
  • Draw a few smaller sunflowers with fewer petals beside the big one.

Questions

Good to know

How do you make sunflower petals look even all the way around?

Draw four first, pointing up, right, down and left. Then add one in each gap. Working in pairs across the circle keeps the spacing balanced.

Why does a sunflower need two layers of petals?

One ring of petals leaves bare spaces between them. The second ring fills those gaps from behind, so the flower looks thick and full.

How big should the center of a sunflower be?

Bigger than you think. On a sunflower, the middle is a large part of the flower, and the petals stay fairly short around it.

How do you draw a sunflower leaf?

Draw a short stalk out from the stem, then a wide heart shape with a pointed tip. Finish with one line down the middle.

How long does it take to draw a sunflower?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
