How to Draw a Canoe

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 8 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 8steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a canoe in 8 steps: draw the top edge, draw the handle and shaft, draw the blade, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 8
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

This canoe starts as a long, gentle smile and grows into a boat with a paddle leaning across it. The paddle goes in before the bottom of the canoe, so the hull's lower curve simply breaks around the blade. It is a good second boat because it is mostly one long curve and one straight line.

Tips

Draw a canoe well

Tips for drawing a canoe

  • Make the canoe about four times as long as it is deep, so it reads as a long boat instead of a bowl.
  • Keep the top edge's dip shallow, with both tips a little higher than the middle and at about the same height.
  • Draw the shaft with one steady stroke. A straight shaft next to the curved hull makes both shapes look clearer.
  • Let the blade be roughly as long as the canoe is deep, widest in its middle and rounded at the end.
  • Run each trim line just under the top edge, close and parallel, stopping where it meets the paddle.

Common mistakes

  • The lower edge is drawn right through the blade, so the paddle looks like it sits behind the canoe. Stop the belly line where it touches the blade, then pick it up again on the other side.
  • The top edge and lower edge never meet, leaving the tips open. Bring the lower curve all the way up so it joins the top edge at each pointed tip.
  • The trim lines wander away from the top edge and look like extra cracks. Keep them the same small distance below the top edge along their whole length, bending as it bends.

Make it your own

  • Tilt the paddle the other way, leaning from the right side of the canoe down to the left.
  • Make the canoe longer and flatter for a racing look, keeping the same small dip in the top edge.
  • Try a different hull color, like red or blue, and keep the paddle brown so it still stands apart.
  • Draw two canoes, one smaller and higher on the page, so it looks farther away.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the canoe look curved and not flat?

Give both edges a curve. The top edge dips gently, and the lower edge swings deeper in the middle, then rises sharply to each tip.

Why draw the paddle before the bottom of the canoe?

With the blade already in place, you know exactly where the belly line stops and starts again. That gap makes the blade sit in front of the boat.

What shape is a canoe paddle blade?

Think of a long, rounded leaf. It is narrow where it joins the shaft, wider in the middle, and rounded at the bottom end.

How can a child draw the long curves more easily?

Mark the two tips with small dots first. Then draw each curve from dot to dot in one slow, smooth stroke.

How long does it take to draw a canoe?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
