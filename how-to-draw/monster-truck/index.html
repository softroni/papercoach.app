How to Draw a Monster Truck

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 14 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 14steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a monster truck in 14 steps: draw the first tire, draw the second tire, draw the hubs, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 14
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Wheels path in Paper Coach

A monster truck is mostly tire. Here the two wheels come first and stay big, and the body sits on top of them like a small hat. You'll draw two circles, give each one a hub and eight short treads, then lay a low bed, cab and hood across the top and finish with color.

Tips

Draw a monster truck well

Tips for drawing a monster truck

  • Make each tire about as wide as the gap between them is long, or wider. The bigger the wheels look next to the body, the more it reads as a monster truck.
  • Draw the hub about one third as wide as the tire. That leaves a thick ring of rubber for the treads to sit on.
  • Point every tread straight at the hub, like spokes that stop short. Start each one at the outer edge and keep them all the same length.
  • Keep the truck body short and flat. The top line runs low over the bed, steps up for the cab, then slopes down to the front.
  • When you draw the arches underneath, leave a clear space between each arch and the top of its tire, so the body looks lifted.

Common mistakes

  • The second tire comes out smaller or lower than the first. Line up its top and bottom with the first tire before you close the circle. Light guide marks help.
  • The treads go all different directions and look like scribbles. Draw the plus sign first, then the X between those lines. Eight evenly spaced treads look neat.
  • The body grows as big as the tires, and the truck looks like a regular car. Keep the body's height smaller than one tire. Small body on giant wheels is the whole look.

Make it your own

  • Swap the purple for any bold color, like red, green or orange, and keep the tires gray so the body stands out.
  • Make the tires even bigger and the body even smaller for a sillier, chunkier truck.
  • Color the hubs to match the body instead of cream, so the truck looks like one matched set.

Questions

Good to know

How do I draw the tires round?

Draw each circle in two halves, left side then right side, and let the ends meet. Go slowly and lightly first, then trace over the best line.

Why are the wheels drawn before the body?

The tires are the biggest part, so they set the size of everything else. Once they are down, it's easy to fit a small body neatly across the top.

How do I make the treads even?

Draw four like a plus sign: top, bottom, left and right. Then add four slanted ones halfway between them, like an X. That spaces all eight evenly.

What makes it look like a monster truck instead of a car?

The size difference. Giant tires, a low small body and a gap above each wheel make the truck look lifted high off the ground.

How long does it take to draw a monster truck?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
