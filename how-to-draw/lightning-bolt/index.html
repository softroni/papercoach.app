How to Draw a Lightning Bolt

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 6 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 6steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a lightning bolt in 6 steps: draw the top, draw the right zig, draw the long slant, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 6
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Sky & Weather path in Paper Coach

A lightning bolt is just a few straight lines that join into one closed shape. You draw the right edge first, all the way down to the point, then come back to the top and draw the left edge to meet it. It takes about two minutes, so it's a quick win for kids and grown-ups alike.

Tips

Draw a lightning bolt well

Tips for drawing a lightning bolt

  • Keep the flat top short, about as wide as your thumb. A short top keeps the bolt narrow and sharp.
  • Make the left edge run alongside the right edge, so the bolt stays about the same width until it narrows into the point.
  • Let the long slant be the longest line in the drawing, about twice as long as the first zig.
  • Use quick, confident strokes. Straight lines that stop hard at each corner make the bolt look crisp.
  • Before the last line, check where the point is, then aim your pencil straight at it.

Common mistakes

  • The two steps sit at different heights, so the bolt looks broken in the middle. Put the left step just a little above the right step, so both edges turn at about the same place.
  • The last line misses the point and leaves a gap, so the shape isn't closed. Slow down near the end and stop right on the tip. A closed shape is also easier to color.
  • The step-out lines run too long and the bolt ends up wide and blocky. Keep each step short, only a little longer than the flat top.

Make it your own

  • Tilt the whole bolt by making the long slant steeper, so it looks like it's striking down fast.
  • Draw two or three bolts side by side in different sizes for a stormy sky.
  • Stretch the long slant much longer for a tall, skinny bolt, or keep it short for a chunky one.
  • Color the bolt with two yellows, a lighter one at the top and a darker one near the point.

Questions

Good to know

Which way should the lightning bolt point?

In this guide the point ends at the bottom. The bolt starts at the top and zigzags down, so it looks like it's falling from the sky.

How do I keep my lightning bolt from looking lopsided?

Draw the two edges like a pair. Each edge has a slant and a short step, so make those turns line up and keep the gap between the edges about the same.

Why does a lightning bolt need to be one closed shape?

When every line connects, the bolt reads as one solid object. It also gives you a clean outline to color inside without the yellow spilling out.

How can I make the point of the bolt look sharp?

Bring the long slant and the last line together at a narrow angle and stop both exactly at the tip. Don't round off the corner.

How long does it take to draw a lightning bolt?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
