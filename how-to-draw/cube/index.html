How to Draw a Cube

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a cube in 7 steps: draw the top, close the top, draw the uprights, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

The cube is the first box on the forms path, and almost every object later is built from it. You draw a flat diamond for the top, drop three straight edges, then close the bottom to match. Three shades of green show which way the light comes from.

Tips

Draw a cube well

Tips for drawing a cube

  • Make the diamond about twice as wide as it is tall. A wide, shallow diamond reads as a top lying flat.
  • Keep all three uprights the same length. Then the two base edges come out parallel to the top edges without any guessing.
  • Draw the uprights straight down the page. Use the side edge of your paper as a guide for what straight down looks like.
  • Before you draw each base edge, look at the top edge right above it and copy its slant.
  • Keep the three greens clearly apart: lightest on top, middle on the left, darkest on the right.

Common mistakes

  • The top is drawn as a tall diamond or a square, so the cube looks tipped toward you. Flatten the diamond. Bring the far and near corners closer together and push the left and right corners out.
  • The base edges slant differently from the top, so the bottom looks bent or twisted. Draw each base edge parallel to the top edge above it, starting from the bottom of the near upright.
  • Two sides get the same shade of green, and the cube goes flat. Give each face its own value. Press harder or add a second layer on the right side until it is clearly darkest.

Make it your own

  • Make a tall box by drawing longer uprights, or a flat tile by drawing short ones. The top diamond stays the same.
  • Color the faces in another color family, such as light, medium and dark blue, keeping the same order of shades.
  • Put the light on the right instead: make the right side medium and the left side darkest.
  • Draw a second cube beside the first with the same diamond shape, so both sit on the same floor.

Questions

Good to know

Why is the top of the cube a diamond and not a square?

You are looking down at the top from an angle, so the square squashes into a diamond. The flatter the diamond, the lower your eye sits.

Why does my cube look flat or wobbly?

Usually the uprights lean or are different lengths. Make all three straight down and equal, and the base will line up with the top.

Which side of the cube should be darkest?

The side facing away from the light. Here the light comes from the upper left, so the top is lightest and the right side is darkest.

How do I check that the bottom of my cube is right?

Each base edge should run the same direction as the top edge above it. If they look like matching pairs of lines, the base is right.

How long does it take to draw a cube?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
