How to Draw a Burger

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a burger in 11 steps: draw the top bun, draw the seeds, draw the lettuce, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

This burger is built from the top down, one layer under the next, so each part rests on the one above it. You will learn to draw a wavy lettuce edge and a little V of cheese that melts over the patty. Those two small details make a simple stack look like a real burger.

Tips

Draw a burger well

Tips for drawing a burger

  • Make the top bun dome wide and fairly low, about twice as wide as it is tall. Every layer below lines up with its width.
  • Draw the sesame seeds in different directions, spread over the dome. Five seeds in a neat row looks stiff.
  • Let the lettuce stick out a little past the bun on both sides, so its round ends show clearly.
  • Keep the five lettuce waves about the same size. Lightly mark the middle wave first, then fit two on each side.
  • Make the patty the thickest layer, a bit taller than the cheese and lettuce, so the burger feels hearty.

Common mistakes

  • The layers drift sideways, so the burger looks like it is sliding over. Line up the left and right ends of each layer under the top bun before you draw the next one.
  • The cheese V is so big it covers most of the patty. Keep the V small and near one corner. It only needs to hang down about halfway into the patty.
  • The bottom bun is drawn as tall as the top dome. Make the bottom bun thinner, with a flat top and only a gentle curve underneath.

Make it your own

  • Make it a double burger by drawing a second patty and cheese slice under the first, each with its own melting V.
  • Draw more or fewer lettuce waves to make the lettuce look frillier or smoother.
  • Add a few more sesame seeds for a busier bun, keeping each one a tiny teardrop.
  • Draw two burgers side by side, one slightly smaller, like a meal for two.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the burger look stacked and not flat?

Draw each layer touching the one above it, with no gaps. Give each layer its own height, with the patty thickest and the cheese thinnest, so the stack reads clearly.

Why does the cheese have a V shape?

The V shows one corner of the square cheese slice drooping over the patty, like it is melting. It is a quick way to show the cheese is soft and warm.

How do I draw wavy lettuce that looks even?

Count the waves as you go, five across. Keep each bump soft and round, about the same width, and start and end them at the round lettuce ends.

Can a young child draw this burger?

Yes. Every part is a simple shape: a dome, a line, waves, a long rounded shape. A parent can draw one layer and let the child copy it underneath.

How long does it take to draw a burger?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
