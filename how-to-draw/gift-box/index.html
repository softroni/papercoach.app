How to Draw a Gift Box

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 12 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 12steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a gift box in 12 steps: draw the bow, draw the lid's top, draw the lid's sides, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 12
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

This gift box is a cube with a shallow lid on top, a ribbon crossing it and a bow in the middle. You draw the bow first, then build the lid and box around it, so nothing gets covered up. It is a friendly way to practice drawing a box from a corner view.

Tips

Draw a gift box well

Tips for drawing a gift box

  • Keep the bow small, about a third as wide as the lid's top. If the loops are too big, there's no room left for the diamond around them.
  • Make the lid's corner lines short, about a quarter as tall as the box's uprights. That way the lid reads as a lid and not a second box.
  • Draw the box's three uprights straight down and the same length. The middle one sits right under the lid's front corner.
  • Each ribbon line on the sides should run parallel to the box's uprights, so the band looks like it hangs straight down.
  • When coloring, keep all the left faces one color and all the right faces another. That simple change makes the box look solid.

Common mistakes

  • Closing the lid's bottom edge with one unbroken line, so the ribbon has nowhere to pass. Stop the line where the ribbon will cross and leave a small gap on each side, about as wide as the ribbon.
  • Making the diamond lopsided, with one side much longer than the other. Keep the left and right points level with each other, and the front point straight below the back point.
  • Ribbon lines that wander or spread apart as they go down the side. Keep the two lines of each band the same distance apart from top to bottom, like train tracks.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: try red sides with a green ribbon, keeping a lighter shade on the left faces and a darker one on the right.
  • Make the box tall and narrow by stretching the three uprights, while the lid stays the same short height.
  • Draw two or three boxes in a stack, each one a little smaller, with the smallest on top.
  • Add simple dots or stripes on the box's sides, keeping them inside the faces and off the ribbon.

Questions

Good to know

Why do you draw the bow before the box?

The bow sits in front of the lid. Drawing it first means you draw the lid's edges around it, so you never have to erase lines that should be hidden.

How do I make the box look 3D?

Show it from a corner so you see the top and two sides. Then color the left and right sides in different shades, with the top lighter.

How wide should the ribbon be?

Keep it narrow, about one tenth of the box's width. Use the same width on the top and on both sides so the band looks continuous.

How do I make the bow look puffy?

Draw each loop as a fat, rounded teardrop that starts at the knot and swells outward. Keep both loops about the same size.

How long does it take to draw a gift box?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
