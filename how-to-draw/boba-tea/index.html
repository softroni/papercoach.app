How to Draw a Boba Tea

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a boba tea in 11 steps: draw the cup, draw the rim, draw the dome, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

This boba tea starts with a cup that is wider at the top than at the bottom. On top go a thin band and a dome for the lid. A straw leans out, and seven round pearls sit in a small pile at the bottom. It takes only a few simple shapes, so it works well for a parent and child drawing side by side.

Tips

Draw a boba tea well

Tips for drawing a boba tea

  • Make the cup's top edge about one and a half times as wide as its bottom edge. That taper is what makes it read as a drink cup.
  • Let the rim stick out a little past both top corners of the cup, so the lid looks like it fits over the cup.
  • Keep the dome about as wide as the rim and no taller than half its width, like a low hill.
  • Draw the straw starting inside the dome and running up past it, about as tall as the cup again.
  • Make all seven pearls about the same size. The bottom four should fill most of the cup's floor, side to side.

Common mistakes

  • The cup sides go straight down, so it looks like a box. Start each side at the top corner and slant it inward as it goes down, so the bottom ends up narrower.
  • The pearls float in the middle of the cup. Set the first four right on the bottom line, then tuck the three upper pearls into the gaps between them.
  • The straw stands straight up and looks stiff. Tilt it to the right, keeping both long sides parallel, and finish it with a flat top.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: a light green tea with a white rim and a yellow straw still keeps the same shapes.
  • Draw two cups side by side with the straws leaning toward each other.
  • Pile the pearls higher by adding a third row of two on top of the three.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the cup look tapered and not lopsided?

Slant both sides inward by the same amount. Check that the bottom line sits centered under the top edge, with equal space left and right.

Why does the lid have two parts?

The thin band is the rim that grips the cup, and the half circle is the dome on top. Drawing them separately makes the lid look raised.

How do I stack the pearls so they look like they are resting?

Put each of the three top pearls over a gap between two bottom pearls. They sit lower that way and look like they settled in place.

Should the straw cross over the dome?

Yes. Start the straw inside the dome and let it rise above the lid, so it looks pushed through the top.

How long does it take to draw a boba tea?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
