How to Draw a Speedboat

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 9 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 9steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a speedboat in 9 steps: draw the deck, draw the hull, draw the windshield, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 9
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

This speedboat starts as one long wedge with a sharp nose, so it looks fast before you add a thing. A lightning bolt down the side and three curling splash loops behind it finish the motion. Red, yellow and blue make it bright and bold.

Tips

Draw a speedboat well

Tips for drawing a speedboat

  • Keep the deck line almost flat at the back and let it lift only near the front. A gentle rise reads as speed, and a steep one looks like a ramp.
  • Make the stern short, about a third of the hull's height at most, so the boat stays long and low.
  • Keep the windshield dome low and small, roughly a quarter of the deck's length, sitting closer to the back than the nose.
  • Draw the bolt's two edges with the same zigzag, one just below the other, so the yellow band stays an even width.
  • Start the splash loops right at the stern and shrink them as they go: big first, then medium, then small.

Common mistakes

  • The nose comes out rounded, so the boat looks like a bathtub. Bring the deck line and the bottom line together at one sharp point. Stop both lines exactly where they meet.
  • The two bolt edges miss each other at the tip and leave a gap. Pick the point first, then aim both edges at that same spot near the nose.
  • All three splash loops end up the same size and look like a fence. Make each loop clearly smaller than the last, so the splash fades away behind the boat.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: a blue hull with a white bolt, or a black hull with an orange bolt.
  • Stretch the wedge longer and lower for a racing boat, or shorten it for a little zippy one.
  • Add more splash loops trailing behind to make the boat look even faster.
  • Give the zigzag two quick turns instead of one for a sharper, wilder bolt.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make a speedboat look fast?

Keep the shape long, low and pointed, with the nose lifted a little. The slanted bar in the windshield, the zigzag bolt and the curling splash behind all point forward and add motion.

Why does the splash go behind the boat and not in front?

A moving boat throws water up from its back as it pushes ahead. Putting the loops at the stern tells the eye which way the boat is going.

How do I draw the lightning bolt so it looks right?

Run a line along the side, make one quick zigzag, then carry on to a point. Repeat the same path just below, and join both lines at that point.

Is this speedboat easy enough for a young child?

Yes. It uses a wedge, a dome, a zigzag and three loops. A parent can draw the wedge lightly first and let the child trace it and add the rest.

How long does it take to draw a speedboat?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
