How to Draw a Cupcake

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a cupcake in 7 steps: draw the wrapper, draw the folds, draw the bottom layer, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach

This cupcake starts with a simple cup shape and ends with a swirl of frosting. The trick is stacking three puffs that get smaller as they go up, so the frosting looks soft and tall. A few fanned lines on the wrapper make it look folded instead of flat.

Tips

Draw a cupcake well

Tips for drawing a cupcake

  • Make the wrapper wider at the top than at the bottom. The slanted sides are what make it look like a cupcake cup and not a box.
  • Space the four fold lines evenly. Keep the middle ones close to straight and let the outer ones lean out to follow the slanted sides.
  • Let the bottom puff hang a little past the wrapper's top edge on both sides, like a cushion spilling over.
  • Each puff should be about three quarters as wide as the one below it. That steady shrinking is what makes the swirl look like it climbs.
  • Draw the top puff's point leaning slightly to one side, like the tip of a wave, so it feels soft rather than sharp.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing all three frosting puffs the same width, which makes the top look like a stack of pillows. Shrink each puff as you go up. The bottom one is the widest, the middle one is smaller, the top one is the smallest.
  • Drawing the fold lines straight down and parallel, so the wrapper looks flat. Fan them out a little. Start them closer together at the bottom and let them spread toward the top edge.
  • Pointing the top puff straight up like a cone. Curve the last line up and over into a gentle tip, like the crest of a small wave.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: try mint green or yellow frosting with a pink or purple wrapper.
  • Add a fourth, even smaller puff on top for a taller swirl.
  • Draw a row of three cupcakes side by side, each with a different frosting color.
  • Use more fold lines, six or seven, for a wrapper with finer ridges.

Questions

Good to know

How do you make cupcake frosting look swirled?

Stack three rounded puffs, each smaller than the one below, and finish the top one in a soft curled point. The shrinking layers and the wave-like tip give the look of a swirl.

How do you draw the lines on a cupcake wrapper?

Draw four lines down the wrapper, from just under its top edge to just above the bottom, fanning them out slightly so they follow the slanted sides.

Why does my cupcake look like a bucket?

The frosting is probably too small or too flat. Make the bottom puff wider than the wrapper's top and give the swirl some height.

Is this cupcake easy enough for kids?

Yes. It is built from a cup shape, a few straight lines, and three bumpy puffs. Children can follow it well, especially if an adult points out that each puff gets smaller.

How long does it take to draw a cupcake?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
