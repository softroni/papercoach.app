How to Draw a Pirate Ship

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 10 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 10steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a pirate ship in 10 steps: draw the deck, draw the hull, add the details, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 10
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

This pirate ship is built from a few bold shapes: a deck line with a raised back, a deep curved hull under it, and two masts with puffed-out sails. The main mast in the middle is the tallest and carries the flag. Once you can draw one sail, the second one is the same shape, only smaller.

Step by step

Draw it in 10 steps

  1. 1Draw the deck

    Begin at the back of the ship. Draw the high stern, step down, and run the deck to the bow.

  2. 2Draw the hull

    Now the big wooden belly. From the stern, curve down, along and up to the bow.

  3. 3Add the details

    Add a plank line along the side. Then a pole pointing out from the bow.

Tips

Draw a pirate ship well

Tips for drawing a pirate ship

  • Make the stern clearly taller than the rest of the deck. That one step up at the back is what makes the boat look like a pirate ship.
  • Draw the hull deep, about as tall as the main sail. A shallow hull makes the ship look like a small rowboat.
  • Keep the top and bottom edges of each sail straight and let only the two sides curve out toward the bow, so the wind seems to push them forward.
  • Keep the flag small and let it stream back toward the stern from the very top of the main mast, so the two sails stay the biggest shapes.
  • Place the front mast between the main mast and the bow, and make its sail about two thirds the size of the main sail.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the sails as triangles or loose rectangles with all four sides bending. Draw two straight, flat edges first, top and bottom, then join them with two curves that bulge the same way, toward the bow.
  • Making the front mast as big as the main mast, so the ship looks crowded. Start its post lower and shorter, and keep its sail smaller. The main mast should stay the tallest thing on the ship.
  • Starting the hull curve partway along the deck, which leaves a gap at the stern or bow. Begin the curve right at the bottom corner of the stern and end it exactly where the deck meets the bow.

Make it your own

  • Stretch the deck and hull longer and add a third mast with a sail between the other two, keeping it smaller than the main one.
  • Draw a few wavy lines under the hull so the ship sits on the sea instead of floating on the page.
  • Swap the colors: try a dark brown hull with light gray sails and a black flag.
  • Draw two ships facing each other, one big and one small, to start a scene.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make a pirate ship look different from a regular boat?

Give it a high stern at the back, a deep round hull, a pole sticking out from the bow, and a flag on the tallest mast. Those shapes together read as a pirate ship.

Which way should the sails bulge?

Both sails bulge toward the bow, the front of the ship. Keep the top and bottom edges straight and curve only the sides, and the sails look full of wind.

Where does the main mast go on the deck?

Put it in the middle of the deck. The smaller front mast goes between it and the bow.

Why do I draw only a short post before the sail?

The sail covers most of the mast. Drawing a short post first, then continuing the mast above the sail, keeps you from erasing lines hidden behind it.

How long does it take to draw a pirate ship?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
