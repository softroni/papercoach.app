How to Draw a Blimp

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a blimp in 11 steps: draw the body, draw the belly, draw the top fin, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the In the Air path in Paper Coach

A blimp is one of the easiest flying machines to draw because its body is just a long, stretched oval. You build that oval in two curves, add a fin above and below the tail, then hang a little cabin underneath. It is a calm, friendly drawing that works well for beginners and kids.

Tips

Draw a blimp well

Tips for drawing a blimp

  • Make the body about three times as long as it is tall. Any rounder and it starts to look like a balloon.
  • Start the top curve and the belly curve at the same nose point, and end both at the same tail point, so the oval closes cleanly.
  • Keep both fins at the back quarter of the body, with the bottom fin lined up right under the top one.
  • Draw the cabin small, about one fifth the length of the body, and place it near the middle of the belly.
  • Space the three windows evenly, leaving a little gap at each end of the cabin.

Common mistakes

  • The fins lean forward toward the nose, so the blimp looks like it is flying backward. Slant both fins back toward the tail, like hair blown by the wind.
  • The seam runs all the way to the nose and tail and crowds the ends. Stop the line a short way before each end so the rounded tips stay clean.
  • The cabin floats below the body with a gap. Let the cabin's top edge touch the belly curve so it looks attached.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: try a red body with blue fins, keeping the cabin a bright color so it stands out.
  • Draw a second, smaller blimp higher on the page so it looks farther away.
  • Stretch the oval longer and add a fourth or fifth window to make a bigger airship cabin.

Questions

Good to know

How do I keep the blimp body from looking lopsided?

Draw the top curve and the belly curve as near mirror images. Pause after the top curve, look at how high it rises, and dip the belly about the same amount below.

Why does the blimp have fins only at the back?

The fins help steer and keep it steady, so they sit near the tail. Keeping them at the back also makes it easy to tell which end is the nose.

What is the line along the middle of the blimp for?

The seam follows the long shape of the body. It makes the flat oval look rounder, like a real puffed-up blimp.

How can I make my blimp look like it is flying?

Leave plenty of empty space around it, and keep the body level or tipped slightly up at the nose. Then it looks like it is floating.

How long does it take to draw a blimp?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
