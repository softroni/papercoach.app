How to Draw a Sailboat

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

Quick answer

Draw a sailboat in 7 steps: draw the deck, draw the hull, draw the plank line, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

Step by step

Each picture shows the lines so far: the faded ones are done, the dark ones are this step’s.

  1. Step 1: Draw the deck

    Every boat here starts with its deck. Draw a long line that lifts at the right, to the bow.

  2. Step 2: Draw the hull

    Now the hull. From the left end, swing down and along. Rise to the bow's tip.

  3. Step 3: Draw the plank line

    A plank line runs along the side. Keep both ends inside the hull.

  4. Step 4: Draw the mast

    Stand the mast on the deck: one tall, straight line going up.

  5. Step 5: Draw the sail

    The sail is a triangle on the mast's left. Slant down from the top, then come straight back.

  6. Step 6: Color the hull

    Color the hull blue.

  7. Step 7: Color the sail

    Color the sail cream.

Questions

Is a sailboat easy to draw?

Yes. This guide splits it into 7 small steps, each a line or two, so you only ever draw one new thing at a time.

How long does it take to draw a sailboat?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
