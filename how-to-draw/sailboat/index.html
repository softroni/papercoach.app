How to Draw a Sailboat

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a sailboat in 7 steps: draw the deck, draw the hull, draw the plank line, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

This sailboat is built from a few simple lines: a deck, a hull that curves under it, a mast and a triangle sail. It is the first boat on this path, so the shapes you learn here come back in the bigger boats later. Take it slowly, and you will have a boat ready to float in a few minutes.

Tips

Draw a sailboat well

Tips for drawing a sailboat

  • Draw the deck line a bit wider than you think. A long, low deck gives the hull and sail room and makes the boat look steady.
  • Let the deck lift only at the right end. That small rise is the bow, and it tells everyone which way the boat is pointing.
  • Keep the bottom of the hull a gentle curve, not a deep bowl. The hull should be shallow next to how tall the mast is.
  • Make the mast about twice as tall as the hull is deep. A tall mast gives the triangle sail a nice, big shape.
  • Start the sail at the very top of the mast so the triangle and the mast share one sharp point.

Common mistakes

  • The plank line runs past the ends of the hull and pokes out into the air. Start and stop the plank line a little inside the hull at both ends, and let it follow the hull's curve.
  • The mast stands off the deck or floats above it. Put your pencil right on the deck line, near the middle, and draw straight up from there.
  • The sail's bottom edge slopes, so the triangle looks crooked. After slanting down from the top, bring the bottom edge straight back across to the mast, level with the deck.

Make it your own

  • Draw a second, smaller sailboat farther back to make a little race on the water.
  • Color the hull red or green instead of blue, and keep the sail cream so it still stands out.
  • Add a few wavy lines under the hull so the boat sits on the sea.
  • Make the sail wider by slanting it farther left, like it is full of wind.

Questions

Good to know

Which side should the sail go on?

Put the triangle on the mast's left, the side away from the bow. The boat points right, so the sail trails behind the mast and the drawing feels balanced.

How do I make the hull look like a boat and not a bowl?

Keep the bottom curve shallow and long, then let it rise up to a point at the bow on the right. The pointed front is what makes it read as a boat.

What is the plank line for?

It shows the wooden planks along the boat's side. One line inside the hull adds a little detail and makes the side look curved instead of flat.

How can a young child draw this sailboat?

Let them think of it as a smile shape for the hull, one stick for the mast and a triangle flag. Draw each part together, one shape at a time.

How long does it take to draw a sailboat?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
