How to Draw a Parachute Crate

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 9 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 9steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a parachute crate in 9 steps: draw the left side, draw the right side, draw the arches, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 9
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the In the Air path in Paper Coach

This parachute crate is made of two simple parts joined by four cords. A wide orange dome sits on top, and a brown box hangs below it. You will learn how lines can link one shape to another, and that idea comes back again and again when you draw things that fly.

Tips

Draw a parachute crate well

Tips for drawing a parachute crate

  • Make the dome about twice as wide as the crate so the canopy looks big enough to slow the fall.
  • Put a light dot for the peak first. Then both sides of the dome curve down from the same dot and match.
  • Keep the three arches close to the same size. Each one starts and stops at a dip so the bottom edge looks even.
  • Leave a clear gap between the canopy and the crate. The cords look longer and the crate seems to hang in the air.
  • Draw the cords with one steady stroke each, from the canopy straight down to the crate.

Common mistakes

  • The two sides of the dome come out lopsided, one tall and one flat. Draw the right side as a mirror of the left, ending at the same height so the dome stands level.
  • The arches curve down like a smile, so the canopy looks closed and heavy. Bend each arch upward into the dome. The bottom edge should look scalloped, like three small bumps pushed up.
  • The outer cords miss the crate and stop in empty space. Draw the box top first if it helps, then run each outer cord right to a top corner.

Make it your own

  • Tilt the whole drawing a little to one side so the crate looks like it is swaying in the wind.
  • Draw two or three crates, one big and close, the others small and higher up, to show some are farther away.
  • Try a taller, narrower box with the same two planks to make a different kind of crate.

Questions

Good to know

How do I keep the parachute dome even?

Start with a dot for the peak and mark where each side will end, at the same height. Then curve each side down to its mark.

Why does the canopy bottom have three arches?

The arches show the soft cloth bunching between the cords. The dips between them are where the inner cords attach, so they also tell you where to draw those lines.

Where should the cords connect to the crate?

The two outer cords go to the top corners of the box. The two inner cords drop from the dips and meet the top line between those corners.

How big should the crate be?

Keep it small next to the canopy, about half its width or less. A small box under a big dome looks right for a parachute.

How long does it take to draw a parachute crate?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
