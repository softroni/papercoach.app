How to Draw a Balcony

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 16 steps, about 5 minutes, on paper.

  • 16steps
  • 5 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a balcony in 16 steps: draw the wall, draw the slab, draw the brackets, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 5 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 16
  • Time: about 5 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Around Town path in Paper Coach

This balcony is built in layers. The wall and stone slab come first. The railing goes in front, and the doors stand behind it, so you learn how one thing can hide part of another. A flower box and a winding vine soften the straight lines, and a few bright colors finish it.

Tips

Draw a balcony well

Tips for drawing a balcony

  • Keep the slab low on the wall, about a quarter of the way up, so the doors have plenty of room to stand tall above the rail.
  • Place the two end bars first, then put the middle bar of the six halfway between them. The bars on each side are easier to space from there.
  • Start each door line at the top of the rail, not at the slab. Starting there makes the rail look like it stands in front of the doors.
  • Make the hood's pointed roof low and wide, a bit wider than the doors, so it looks like a shelter over them.
  • Let the vine's stem bend left and right a few times as it climbs. A gentle wave looks more alive than a straight line.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the door lines all the way down through the railing to the slab. Stop the door lines where they meet the rail. The rail hides the bottom of the doors.
  • Bunching the bars together at one end so the gaps don't match. Mark six light dots along the rail first, evenly spread, then drop a bar from each dot.
  • Drawing both brackets the same way, so they look like plain boxes under the slab. Keep the straight edge toward the middle and the curved edge facing out on each side, like a mirror image.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors: a pale blue wall with dark red doors, or a white wall with blue doors, keeping the flowers red so they still stand out.
  • Let the vine climb higher and add a third round flower near the hood.
  • Give the bush in the flower box more bumps and more red dots so it looks like it is overflowing.
  • Draw two balconies side by side on one long wall, each with its own doors and rail, like a row of town houses.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the railing look like it is in front of the doors?

Draw the railing before the doors, then start the door lines from the top rail. Since the doors never cross the rail, it reads as standing in front of them.

How tall should the doors be?

Make them reach most of the way up the wall, leaving room above for the band and the pointed hood. Tall, narrow doors make the balcony feel grand.

Why are the brackets curved on one side?

Real balcony brackets often curve outward to look like they are holding the slab up. The curved outer edge and straight inner edge give that look with just two lines.

How can I keep the vine from looking messy?

Draw one smooth winding stem first. Then add just the two round flowers and four leaves, two on each side, spaced out along it.

How long does it take to draw a balcony?

About 5 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
