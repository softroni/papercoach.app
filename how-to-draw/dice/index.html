How to Draw Dice

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 12 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 12steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw dice in 12 steps: draw the top edges, draw the sides, draw the bottom edges, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 12
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

A die is a great first object after a plain box, because the dots show you which way each face is turned. You build a six-sided outline, split it into three faces with a Y shape, then squash each dot to match its face. If the dots look flat on the surface, the cube looks solid.

Tips

Draw dice well

Tips for drawing dice

  • Keep the two sides the same length and straight up and down. If one runs longer, the cube will look like it is tipping.
  • Make the V wide and shallow, about the same slope as the two top edges above it. The top then reads as a flat diamond.
  • Place the top dot right in the middle of the diamond, and make it about twice as wide as it is tall.
  • On the left face, put one dot near the upper corner and one near the lower corner, so they sit on a slanted diagonal.
  • Space the three dots on the right face evenly, with the middle dot at the center of the face.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing every dot as a perfect circle, which makes the dots seem to float in front of the cube. Squash each dot into an oval and tilt it along the slope of its face's edges.
  • Tilting the dots on the left and right faces the same way. Check each face on its own. Left face dots lean one way, and right face dots lean the opposite way.
  • Letting the middle line drift to one side, so one face gets much wider than the other. Start at the point of the V and go straight down to the bottom corner, keeping both faces about equal.

Make it your own

  • Swap the colors around, with a cream right face and an orange top, and keep the dots black.
  • Try other numbers from a real die, such as four dots on the left face or five on the right.
  • Draw two dice side by side, each showing different numbers on its three faces.
  • Make the dots the light color and the faces dark, so the numbers stand out the other way around.

Questions

Good to know

How do you draw a die so it looks 3D?

Show three faces at once: a diamond top and two side faces that meet at a center line. Then squash the dots so they lean with each face. Those slanted dots do most of the work.

Why do the dots on a die look like ovals?

Each face is turned away from you, so a round dot looks narrower in one direction. That is why the top dot lies flat and the side dots lean along their faces.

What numbers should the three faces of a die show?

This drawing shows one on top, two on the left and three on the right. It keeps the dot count low, so you can focus on getting each oval to follow its face.

How do I keep the edges of the cube straight?

Draw each edge in one steady stroke, moving from your arm instead of your fingers. Make sure the matching edges slope the same way, like the left top edge and the right bottom edge.

How long does it take to draw dice?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
