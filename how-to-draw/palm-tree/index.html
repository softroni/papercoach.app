How to Draw a Palm Tree

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a palm tree in 11 steps: draw the trunk, finish the trunk, draw the rings, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Plants path in Paper Coach

This palm tree uses two simple ideas. The trunk is two curves that lean right together, and every leaf is an arch with a zigzag under it. Draw that leaf five times, long at the sides and short where it droops, and you have a full crown in green, dark green and brown.

Tips

Draw a palm tree well

Tips for drawing a palm tree

  • Keep the two trunk curves wide apart at the ground and close together at the top. The trunk will taper like a real palm.
  • Draw each arch in one smooth pass before adding any teeth. The arch sets the leaf's shape, and the zigzag only follows it back.
  • Make the two side leaves the longest ones. The two drooping leaves should be clearly shorter, hanging below them.
  • Start all five leaves from the same spot at the top of the trunk, so the crown looks like it grows from one point.
  • Leave a small gap where each ring meets the trunk's edges. That gap helps the trunk look round.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the trunk straight up and down. Bend both curves the same way, toward the right, so the whole trunk leans as one piece.
  • Zigzag teeth that are tiny on one leaf and huge on the next. Keep the teeth about the same size on every leaf, a little smaller near each tip.
  • Drawing the rings as straight lines across the trunk. Curve each ring down in the middle like a small smile, and space all four evenly up the trunk.

Make it your own

  • Bend both trunk curves more so the palm leans far over, as if a strong wind is pushing it.
  • Swap the greens: make the side leaves light green and the top and drooping leaves dark green.
  • Add a sixth or seventh leaf between the others for a fuller crown, using the same arch and zigzag.
  • Draw a second, smaller palm leaning the other way beside the first, so the two crowns almost touch.

Questions

Good to know

How do you draw palm tree leaves?

Draw each leaf as an arch with a zigzag under it. Curve smoothly out to the tip, then saw your way back to the trunk. Change the length and direction for each of the five leaves.

How do you make a palm trunk look curved instead of stiff?

Use two curves that bend the same way, wide apart at the bottom and closer at the top. A short flat line joins them at the ground.

What are the lines on a palm tree trunk?

They are rings left where old leaves once grew. Here, four short curved lines show them. Their smile shape makes the trunk look round.

Why are some palm leaves darker green than others?

Two greens keep the leaves apart. The long side leaves are dark green, so they stand out from the top leaf and the drooping leaves beside them.

How long does it take to draw a palm tree?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
