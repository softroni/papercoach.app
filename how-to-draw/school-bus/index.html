How to Draw a School Bus

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a school bus in 11 steps: draw the box, draw the floor, draw two windows, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Wheels path in Paper Coach

A school bus is a good first vehicle because it starts as one long box. Add a row of four soft-cornered windows, a stripe below them, and two round wheels tucked into arches. Then fill it with yellow, blue, black and gray.

Tips

Draw a school bus well

Tips for drawing a school bus

  • Make the box about three times as wide as it is tall. A long, low shape reads as a bus right away.
  • Place each wheel arch a short way in from the box's ends, not right at the corners, so the bus has a little bumper on each side.
  • Leave about the same small gap between each window. Even spacing keeps the row of four looking neat.
  • Keep the stripe close under the windows and parallel to the roof, so it looks like it wraps along the side.
  • Draw each hub small, about a third as wide as its tire, so the black tire still shows around it.

Common mistakes

  • The windows get bigger or smaller as you move to the right, so the last one doesn't fit. Lightly mark four even spots along the top before drawing, then size each window to fit its spot.
  • The tire fills the arch completely and touches its edge, so the wheel looks stuck. Draw the circle a bit smaller than the arch, leaving a thin even gap all the way around.
  • The stripe runs all the way to the box's edges and looks like a crack. Start and stop the stripe a little in from each end of the bus.

Make it your own

  • Stretch the box longer and add a fifth or sixth window for a bigger bus.
  • Color the stripe a darker shade than the yellow body so it stands out along the side.
  • Draw two or three buses in a row, each a little smaller, like a line of buses parked at school.
  • Try a different color for the body, such as red or green, to make a city bus instead.

Questions

Good to know

How do I keep the bus box from looking crooked?

Draw the roof line first as one slow, level stroke, then drop the two sides straight down. Check that both sides are the same height before you add the floor.

Why does the bus only have two wheels?

You are looking at the bus from the side, so the wheels on the far side hide behind the near ones. Two wheels, one near each end, is what you see.

How do I draw round windows with soft corners?

Draw a square, but curve each corner instead of making a sharp point. Think of a cushion or a slice of toast.

Can a young child draw this school bus?

Yes. Every part is a box, a square or a circle. A parent can draw the long box and let the child add windows and wheels.

How long does it take to draw a school bus?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
