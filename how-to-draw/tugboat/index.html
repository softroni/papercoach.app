How to Draw a Tugboat

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a tugboat in 11 steps: draw the bumper's top, close the bumper, draw the hull, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

A tugboat is short, tough and a little round, and that is what makes it fun to draw. You start with a long pill-shaped bumper, hang a deep hull under it, then stack a cabin and a smokestack on top. Four bright colors finish it.

Tips

Draw a tugboat well

Tips for drawing a tugboat

  • Make the bumper long and thick, about as wide as the whole boat. Everything else hangs from it or sits on it.
  • Let the hull be deep, nearly as tall as the cabin. A shallow hull makes the boat look like a canoe, not a tug.
  • Keep the cabin shorter than the bumper and set it toward the back, so the bow end stays open.
  • Make the smokestack narrow, about a third as wide as the cabin, and put the band close to its top.
  • Place the round window on the bow side of the cabin so the boat looks like it is heading forward.

Common mistakes

  • The bumper comes out as two thin lines that never meet. Round off the end and bring the line back underneath so it closes like a pill.
  • The hull's bow curve stops short of the bumper and leaves a gap. Sweep the curve all the way up until it touches the bumper's bow end.
  • The cabin floats above the boat with space below it. Start the cabin's sides right on the bumper's top line so it stands on it.

Make it your own

  • Try a taller smokestack with two bands instead of one.
  • Swap the colors: a blue hull, a white cabin and a red stack still read as a tugboat.
  • Add a second round window beside the first, spaced evenly along the cabin.
  • Draw a few wavy lines under the hull so the tug sits on water.

Questions

Good to know

What makes a tugboat look different from other boats?

Its short, stubby shape. A thick bumper all around, a deep hull, and a cabin with a smokestack sitting high make it look strong and compact.

Which end of the tugboat is the bow?

The bow is the front, where the bumper rises a little and the hull sweeps up in a big curve. The round window faces that way.

Why does the tugboat have a bumper?

Tugboats push and pull bigger ships, so a thick bumper protects them. In the drawing, it is the long pill shape that everything else attaches to.

How do I keep the smokestack straight?

Draw both sides going straight up from the roof, the same height, then join them across the top. Add the band parallel to that top line.

How long does it take to draw a tugboat?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
