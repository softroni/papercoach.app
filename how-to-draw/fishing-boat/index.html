How to Draw a Fishing Boat

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 11 steps, about 3 minutes, on paper.

  • 11steps
  • 3 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a fishing boat in 11 steps: draw the deck, draw the hull, draw the plank line, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 3 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 11
  • Time: about 3 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the On the Water path in Paper Coach

This little fishing boat is built from simple pieces: a long deck line, a rounded hull hanging under it, and a boxy cabin with a roof on top. A single slanted line becomes the rod, and a tiny J becomes the hook. It is a calm, quick drawing that works well for a parent and child sitting side by side.

Tips

Draw a fishing boat well

Tips for drawing a fishing boat

  • Keep the deck line flat for most of its length and only lift it in the last quarter, so the bow comes to a clear point on the right.
  • Make the hull about as deep as the cabin is tall. A shallow hull makes the boat look like it could tip over.
  • Place the cabin a little left of center. That leaves open deck at the bow and room at the stern for the rod.
  • Let the roof stick out past both cabin walls by a small, even amount, like a lid that is slightly too big.
  • Draw the fishing line truly straight down from the rod tip, as if a weight were pulling it. A slanted line looks windblown.

Common mistakes

  • The hull's bottom curve ends below the bow instead of meeting it at the point. Aim the curve up so it finishes exactly at the tip of the deck line, closing the shape into one pointed end.
  • The rod starts in the cabin or points toward the bow. Start the rod on the deck behind the cabin and angle it up and to the left, out over the back of the boat.
  • The plank line runs all the way to both ends and crowds the hull edge. Stop it a little short at each end and keep it halfway between the deck and the bottom of the hull.

Make it your own

  • Flip the drawing so the bow points left and the rod leans out to the right.
  • Make the cabin taller and narrower with two windows stacked one above the other.
  • Try other colors for the hull, such as red or navy, and keep the yellow cabin so it still stands out.
  • Draw the rod longer so the line drops well past the bottom of the hull, as if fishing in deep water.

Questions

Good to know

How do I make the bow look pointed instead of round?

Bring the deck line and the hull curve together at one sharp tip. Curve the deck up gently, then let the hull rise steeply to meet it, so the two lines form a narrow point.

Where should the cabin sit on the boat?

Stand both walls right on the deck line, a bit left of the middle. Keep the cabin shorter than the boat is long, about a third of the deck's length works well.

How do I draw the hook so it looks right?

At the bottom of the fishing line, curve the end around and up into a small J. Keep it tiny, about the width of the window's corner curve.

Why is the roof wider than the cabin?

The overhang makes it read as a roof and not just another wall. A slab with round ends that pokes out a little on each side gives the cabin a finished top.

How long does it take to draw a fishing boat?

About 3 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
