How to Draw a Sphere

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a sphere in 7 steps: draw the left side, draw the right side, draw the dark side, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

A sphere is the simplest form, and it is one of the best for learning how light works. You draw one circle in two halves. Then you shade the lower right with five slanted lines and lay a flat oval shadow beside it. Those few marks make a flat circle look round and solid.

Tips

Draw a sphere well

Tips for drawing a sphere

  • Start both arcs at the same top point and end them at the same bottom point. That keeps the two halves even, so the ball stays round.
  • Slant all five hatch lines the same way and space them evenly. Parallel lines look like one shaded area. Lines at different angles look like scratches.
  • Keep the hatching in the lower right quarter of the ball. Leave the upper left clean, because that is where the light hits.
  • Make the shadow oval about as wide as the ball but only a thin sliver tall. A flat oval looks like it lies on the ground.
  • Start the shadow right under the ball so the two touch. A gap between them makes the ball look like it is floating.

Common mistakes

  • The hatch lines poke out past the edge of the circle. Stop each line at the outline. The lines near the bottom are shorter because the curve gets narrower there.
  • The shadow ends up on the left, or on a different side from the hatching. The light comes from the upper left. Put the dark side and the shadow on the right, so they line up.
  • The shadow is drawn as a round circle, like a second ball. Squash it into a long, flat oval that is much wider than it is tall.

Make it your own

  • Pick another color for the ball, like blue or green. Keep the shadow gray so the ball still sits on the ground.
  • Move the light to the upper right. Put the hatching on the lower left and let the shadow fall to the left.
  • Draw two or three spheres of different sizes in a row. Point every shadow the same way so they share one light.
  • Add a few more hatch lines closer together for a darker side, or space them wider for softer shading.

Questions

Good to know

How do I draw a round circle freehand?

Draw it in two arcs. Mark a top point, swing down the left side, then go back to the top and swing down the right. Draw lightly first, then go over the best line.

Why does the shadow go to the right of the ball?

The light shines from the upper left. The ball blocks it, so the shadow lands on the far side, which is the right. The dark side of the ball faces the same way.

What is hatching?

Hatching means shading with rows of short lines that run the same way. Lines placed close together look darker. Lines spread apart look lighter. Here, five slanted lines make the dark side.

How do I make a sphere look round instead of flat?

Shade only one side and leave the lit side plain. Then add a shadow on the ground. That change from light to dark tells the eye the circle is a ball.

How long does it take to draw a sphere?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
