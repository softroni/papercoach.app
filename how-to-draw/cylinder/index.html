How to Draw a Cylinder

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 6 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 6steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a cylinder in 6 steps: draw the top, close the top, draw the sides, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 6
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Forms path in Paper Coach

A cylinder is a circle that has grown tall, like a can or a candle. You build it from a flat oval on top, two matching straight sides, and a bottom curve that copies the front of the oval. Once this shape feels easy, many round objects around you get easier too.

Tips

Draw a cylinder well

Tips for drawing a cylinder

  • Keep the top oval wide and flat, about three times wider than it is tall. A rounder oval makes the cylinder look like it is tipping toward you.
  • Start each side at the far left and far right ends of the oval, where it is widest. Starting inside those points leaves little bumps at the top corners.
  • Make the bottom curve bend the same way as the front of the oval, and about as deep. When the two curves match, the cylinder stands up straight.
  • Draw the oval lightly first. It is much easier to fix its shape before the sides are hanging from it.
  • Keep the yellow inside the oval and the orange below it. The clean edge between them makes the top read as a flat face.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the bottom as a straight line across. Let the bottom sag down in the middle, just like the front edge of the top oval.
  • The two sides lean in or out, or end at different heights. Draw both sides straight down and stop them level with each other before you add the bottom curve.
  • The oval ends come to sharp points, like a lemon. Round off each end of the oval as the line turns back, so it stays smooth all the way around.

Make it your own

  • Make the sides much longer for a tall, thin tube, or very short for a flat shape like a coin.
  • Try new colors, such as a blue body with a light blue top, keeping the top the lighter one.
  • Draw two or three cylinders of different heights side by side, each with the same flat oval on top.

Questions

Good to know

Why is the top of a cylinder an oval instead of a circle?

The top really is a circle, but you see it from the side. Tilted away from you, it looks squashed into a flat oval.

Why does the bottom of a cylinder curve instead of being flat?

The bottom is a circle too. You only see its front half, so it shows as a curve sagging the same way as the front of the top oval.

How do I get both sides of the cylinder the same length?

Before drawing the sides, put a small dot below each end of the oval at the same height. Then draw each side straight down to its dot.

Why is the top colored lighter than the body?

The top faces up toward the light, so it looks brighter. A lighter top and darker body show the two faces apart and make the cylinder look solid.

How long does it take to draw a cylinder?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
