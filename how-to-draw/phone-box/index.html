How to Draw a Phone Box

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 13 steps, about 4 minutes, on paper.

  • 13steps
  • 4 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a phone box in 13 steps: draw the corner, draw the sides, draw the bands, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 4 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 13
  • Time: about 4 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Around Town path in Paper Coach

A phone box is a tall box seen from its corner, so both sides shrink as they go back. Once you get that one tilt right, the bands, panes and base all follow it. The shaded brown side next to the bright red one is what makes the box look solid.

Tips

Draw a phone box well

Tips for drawing a phone box

  • Make the corner line the longest upright in the drawing. Every other edge of the box is shorter because it sits farther away from you.
  • Keep each side's top and bottom lines heading toward the same far point. If you extend them past the box, they should almost meet.
  • Draw the panes as small slanted boxes that follow the side's tilt, not as flat squares. Leave a thin gap of red between them.
  • Draw the tree only where it shows beside the right side. Stop its bumpy edge where it touches the box so it sits behind it.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the tops and bottoms of the sides as flat, level lines. Tilt them. Tops slope down and bottoms slope up as they move away from the corner, so each side gets shorter at its far edge.
  • Making the right-side panes the same width as the left ones. Squeeze the far panes a little narrower. That small change tells the eye the right side turns away from you.
  • Coloring both sides the same red. Keep the left side red and make the right side brown. The darker side reads as shade and gives the box its corner.

Make it your own

  • Turn the box the other way, with the long side on the right and the short side on the left, and move the shade to the left.
  • Swap the round tree for a taller, narrower one that rises above the domed roof.
  • Draw two phone boxes side by side, the second one a bit smaller and farther back.
  • Keep the bands blank, or letter a short word into the cream band in your own handwriting.

Questions

Good to know

Why does a phone box look wrong when I draw it straight on?

Seen flat from the front, it's only a rectangle. Showing the corner, with two sides sloping away, gives it depth and makes it look like a real box standing on the street.

How do I get the domed roof to fit the box?

Start and end the curve exactly at the two far top corners. Let it rise gently in the middle, then set the small knob right at its highest point.

How do I keep twelve panes even?

Split each side into three rows first, then cut each row in two. Do the near side, then copy the same rows on the far side, just narrower.

Why is one side brown instead of red?

That side is turned away from the light, so it sits in shade. A darker color there makes the near corner pop forward and the box feel solid.

How long does it take to draw a phone box?

About 4 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
