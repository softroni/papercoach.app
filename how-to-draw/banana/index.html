How to Draw a Banana

An easy step-by-step drawing for kids and beginners: 7 steps, about 2 minutes, on paper.

  • 7steps
  • 2 minabout
  • Colorat the end

Quick answer

Draw a banana in 7 steps: draw the top edge, draw the bottom edge, draw the stem, then carry on one line at a time and color it in. It takes about 2 minutes with a pencil or a pen.

  • Steps: 7
  • Time: about 2 minutes
  • You need: paper, a pencil or a pen, something to color with
  • From: the Fruits path in Paper Coach

A banana is really two curves that start at the same left end, one shallow on top and one deeper below. The space between them gives the fruit its thickness. A small square stem, a tiny hook at the other end and two long ridge lines make it read as a banana and not a crescent moon.

Tips

Draw a banana well

Tips for drawing a banana

  • Keep the top curve shallow. A small dip in the middle leaves room for the deeper bottom curve to make the banana fat.
  • Bring the bottom line up to the right end of the top line, so the two curves narrow as they meet.
  • Keep the stem small. It should be about as wide as the left end of the banana and no wider.
  • Let each ridge line copy the bottom curve, keeping an even gap from it. If the bottom curve bends, the ridge bends the same way.
  • Color the yellow with light pressure so the two ridge lines still show through it.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing the top and bottom curves at the same depth, which leaves a thin strip. Swing the bottom line much lower than the top line, so the banana is widest in the middle.
  • Drawing the ridge lines straight across the inside of the banana. Curve them along the belly from one end toward the other, so they bend with the bottom edge.
  • Making the tip as big as the stem. Keep the hook short and tiny. The stem is the larger end, and the tip is only a small dark point.

Make it your own

  • Flip the drawing so the stem sits on the right and the curve rises to the left.
  • Dip both curves deeper for a more bent banana, keeping the bottom line lower than the top line.
  • Draw three bananas side by side in different sizes, using the same steps for each one.
  • Stop before coloring and keep it as a line drawing, with the ridges as the only inside detail.

Questions

Good to know

How do you make a banana look curved?

Use two curves that bend the same way, with the bottom one deeper. The banana gets wider in the middle and narrower at both ends, which gives it that bend.

Why does my banana look like a crescent moon?

A moon is just two curves with pointed ends. The square stem on the left, the small hook on the right and the two ridge lines are what turn that shape into a banana.

What are the lines on a banana?

They are ridges, the long edges that run along the skin. Two lines inside the shape that follow the belly are enough to show them.

How can a young child draw a banana?

Think of the top line as a wide, gentle smile. Then draw a bigger smile under it that joins at the ends. Add a small square stem on the left.

How long does it take to draw a banana?

About 2 minutes for the lines, and a few more to color it in.

Can kids follow this guide?

Yes, children from about six can follow it on their own, and younger ones with a grown-up beside them.

What do I need?

Paper and a pencil or a pen, and crayons, markers or colored pencils for the colors. An eraser helps but isn’t needed.
