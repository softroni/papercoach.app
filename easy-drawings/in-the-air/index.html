Easy Aircraft Drawings

10 drawings from the In the Air path in Paper Coach, easiest first, each one line at a time on paper.

These ten drawings start with the simplest things that fly and get harder bit by bit. The Hot Air Balloon, Paper Airplane and Parachute Crate come first, and each is built from a few basic shapes. Next come the Helicopter, Drone and Jet, and you finish with Balloon Over the Hill, where smaller things look farther away.

Questions

Good to know

Which drawing should we start with?

Start with the Hot Air Balloon, then the Paper Airplane and the Parachute Crate. They are the easiest three. The later drawings reuse what you learn in them, so it helps to do these first.

How can a drawing show that something is far away?

Draw it smaller. In Balloon Over the Hill, a big shape looks close and a small one looks far off. That one change makes the sky feel deep, with no extra detail needed.

Is this good for a parent and child to draw together?

Yes. Each drawing is split into short steps, so you can take turns. One person draws a step, the other draws the next, and you compare your drawings at the end.

Draw the whole path with Lina

In Paper Coach, Lina draws each line of every In the Air drawing on the screen, then waits while you draw it on paper. The first 3 are free.
