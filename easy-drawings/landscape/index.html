Questions

Good to know

How do you make a landscape drawing look deep?

Let near shapes overlap the far ones. Draw things smaller as they go back, and make distant hills paler. A road that narrows toward the horizon also pulls the eye into the picture.

Which landscape should a beginner draw first?

Start with Rolling Hills. It is a few curved lines, each hill partly hidden by the one in front. Then try Winding River and Lake Reflection before moving on to mountains and cliffs.

How do you draw a road going to the horizon?

Start with two lines far apart at the bottom of the page. Bring them closer as they climb until they almost meet at the horizon line. Country Road walks through it step by step.
