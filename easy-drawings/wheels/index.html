Questions

Good to know

Which vehicle should we draw first?

Start with the school bus. Its body is one big box, so it is the easiest to get right. The car and the dump truck come next, and the later drawings reuse those same shapes.

How do I keep the wheels the same size?

Draw one wheel, then look at its width before you draw the next one beside it. Keep your lines light, so a wheel that comes out too big is easy to erase and redo.

Is this a good fit for drawing with a young child?

Yes. The first drawings use simple boxes and circles that a small child can follow. A parent can draw the same vehicle right next to them, one step at a time, and compare.
