Questions

Good to know

Which boat should a beginner draw first?

Start with the Sailboat. It is one sail on a hull, so you only need a few simple shapes. The Canoe and the Tugboat come next. They are still short and easy.

Are these boat drawings good for kids?

Yes. Each guide breaks a boat into small steps, one shape at a time. A parent and child can draw side by side and begin with the first three, which are the easiest.

Should I draw the easy boats before the Harbor?

It helps. Each later drawing builds on the earlier ones. The shapes you practice on the first boats come back in bigger ones like the Pirate Ship and the Cruise Ship, and then in the Harbor scene.
