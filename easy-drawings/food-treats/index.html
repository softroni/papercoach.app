Easy Food Drawings

10 drawings from the Food & Treats path in Paper Coach, easiest first, each one line at a time on paper.

Start with treats built from a few simple shapes, like a donut, a popsicle and a burger. Then move on to food with more layers and parts, such as boba tea, a cupcake and a ramen bowl. The last drawing puts several snacks together on one snack tray.

Questions

Good to know

Which food drawing should I start with?

Start with the donut. It is the easiest of the ten. The popsicle and the burger come next. The later drawings reuse what you practice in those first three.

Are these drawings good to do with a child?

Yes. Each drawing is split into small steps, one part at a time. A parent and child can draw side by side on their own paper and compare after every step.

Why does the path end with a snack tray?

The snack tray brings several foods into one picture. You place pieces next to each other and keep their sizes sensible. The earlier drawings prepare you for that.

Draw the whole path with Lina

In Paper Coach, Lina draws each line of every Food & Treats drawing on the screen, then waits while you draw it on paper. The first 3 are free.
