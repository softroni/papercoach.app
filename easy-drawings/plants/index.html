Easy Tree and Flower Drawings

10 drawings from the Plants path in Paper Coach, easiest first, each one line at a time on paper.

Questions

Good to know

Which plant drawing should I start with?

Start with the pine tree. It is made of stacked triangles, so it only takes a few minutes. Then draw the tulip and the cactus. The later drawings build on what those first three teach.

Can a young child draw these plants?

Yes. The first three use big, simple shapes a child can copy. A parent can draw the same plant on a separate sheet, one step at a time, and you can compare when you both finish.

What do I need to draw them?

A pencil, an eraser and plain paper are enough. Press lightly at first so mistakes are easy to erase, then go over the lines you want to keep.
