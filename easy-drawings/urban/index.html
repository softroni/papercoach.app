10 drawings from the Around Town path in Paper Coach, easiest first, each one line at a time on paper.

Questions

Good to know

Do I have to draw every brick and roof tile?

No. A few bricks in one corner of a wall, or a handful of tiles along a roofline, tell the eye the rest is there. Leaving most of the surface plain keeps the drawing light and clear.

Which drawing should we start with?

Start with the fire hydrant, the shop front or the balcony. They are the easiest three, and the later drawings, like the street corner and the café, build on what they teach.

Is this path good for drawing with a child?

It is marked advanced, so a young child may enjoy the first three most. A parent can take a bigger scene, like the canal bridge or the rooftops, while the child draws the fire hydrant beside them.
