Good to know

What is a scene in Paper Coach?

A whole picture, drawn the way every lesson is: Lina shows each line, you draw it on paper, one part at a time, and then you color it in.

Which scenes are free?

One on every path, marked “Free in the app” here. The others come with Premium.

Do I need to draw the lessons first?

It helps. Each scene names the lessons it is built from, so a drawing you have practiced turns up again as part of a bigger picture.

Draw a whole picture with Lina

Every scene in Paper Coach is drawn line by line, the same way as the lessons, and finished in color.
