About

What is Paper Coach?

The facts, in one place.

  • Paper Coach is an app for iPhone and iPad that teaches drawing step by step, on real paper, made by Softroni.
  • Each lesson shows one line at a time, drawn slowly on the screen, then waits while the learner draws it with a pencil or pen and taps “I drew it”.
  • There are 120 lessons on 12 paths (Plants, Fruits, Sky & Weather, Food & Treats, Forms, Wheels, On the Water, In the Air, Space, Landscape, Around Town, Fantasy Objects), grouped in three levels: Starter, Core and Advanced.
  • Scenes are whole pictures drawn the same way, in short chapters.
  • Lina, the coach, is a cartoon artist who says each step aloud in a computer-generated voice; a switch in Settings turns her voice off, and every step is also written on screen.
  • It is for kids from about six, teens and grown-ups, especially people who say they can’t draw.
  • It is free to download. The first three lessons of every path, one scene per path and the drawing of the day are free; Premium opens everything, by the week, by the year, or with one Lifetime payment, and works with Family Sharing.
  • For families: several learners can share a device, each with their own progress and sketchbook; a grown-up can get an evening note about what a child drew; prices are behind a grown-ups’ check.
  • No account is needed. Lessons are on the device, and photos of finished drawings stay in the app’s sketchbook.
  • The app is in English.
