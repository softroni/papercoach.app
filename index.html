Learn to draw, one line at a time

Paper Coach shows you each line of a drawing, then waits while you draw it on real paper. Step by step, the picture appears in your own hand. For kids and grown-ups who say they can’t draw.

How it works

  1. Watch a line. Lina draws one line on the screen, slowly, so you see where it starts, where it goes and where it stops.
  2. Draw it on paper. Copy it onto your page with a pencil or a pen, then tap “I drew it”. Nothing moves on until you do.
  3. Finish a picture. A few minutes later the whole drawing is on your paper. Keep a photo of it in your sketchbook.

Meet Lina, your drawing coach

Lina is the artist inside Paper Coach. She draws each line slowly on the screen, says in a sentence or two where it starts and where it goes, then waits while you draw it on your paper. She never hurries you and never keeps score, and when the picture is done she has one last thing to say about it.

Her voice is computer-generated. A switch in the app’s Settings turns it off, and every step is written on the screen as well.

What you can draw

Plants

Trees, flowers and potted plants, from stacked triangles to a twisted bonsai, ending on a windowsill garden.

Fruits

Round, curved and clustered shapes, ending with four fruits overlapping in a bowl.

Sky & Weather

From a sun made of a circle and rays to a storm over the mountains: simple shapes first, then overlap, then a whole scene.

Food & Treats

Treats first, then stacked and layered food, ending with a full snack tray.

Forms

The skills path: boxes, cylinders, cones and spheres, then everyday objects built from them, with one light direction.

Wheels

Everything that rolls, from a box-shaped bus to an ambulance with its cross and roof light.

On the Water

Boats and ships, from one sail on a hull to a harbour scene.

In the Air

Things that fly, from a paper airplane to a busy sky that uses size for depth.

Space

Moons, planets and spacecraft, ending with the solar system drawn on orbit ellipses.

Landscape

Depth without buildings: overlap, size, paler distance, reflections and a road to the horizon, ending with a full mountain valley.

Around Town

The town as you see it: shop fronts, balconies and bridges, textures you suggest instead of copying, a tiled rooftop, ending with a café and its table for two.

Fantasy Objects

Treasures from a fantasy world: a potion, a jeweled crown and an hourglass, then a sword, a spellbook and a cauldron, ending with an open treasure chest.

Questions

What is Paper Coach?

An iPhone and iPad app that teaches drawing step by step, on real paper. Lina, the coach, draws one line on the screen and waits while you draw it on your page. There are 120 lessons on 12 paths, from a pine tree to a race car, and whole scenes drawn in short chapters.

Who is it for?

Anyone who says they can’t draw: kids from about six, teens and grown-ups. The first lessons of every path are simple, and the later ones build on them.

Do I need a tablet or a stylus?

No. You draw with a pencil or a pen on paper. The phone or iPad only shows you the next line.

Is it free?

It is free to download. The first three lessons of every path, one scene on every path and the drawing of the day are free. Premium opens every lesson, by the week, by the year, or with one Lifetime payment.

How long does a lesson take?

Most take five to ten minutes, and each one ends with a finished drawing on your paper.
